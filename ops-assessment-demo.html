Diagnostic Summary: Summit Roofing is running on operator instinct, not systems. Revenue has grown 40% in two years but gross margin has compressed from 47% to 41% — the gap lives in untracked job costs and unbilled change orders. The owner is approving every estimate and can't take a week off. Technology is underutilized across the board. The business has the revenue to fix this; it needs a sequenced plan and someone to hold it accountable.

Items below can be completed within 30 days with minimal outside help. Sequenced by impact-to-effort ratio.
1
Turn on ServiceTitan job costing templates
Build 4 job templates (shingle, metal, flat, repair) with labor hours, material estimates, and target GM%. Costs $0 — already in your subscription. First week of data reveals your real margin by job type.
Effort: 6 hrsImpact: HIGHOwner: Office Manager
2
Build a 13-week rolling cash flow in Google Sheets
Pull AR aging from QBO weekly. Map projected inflows against payroll dates and supplier draws. Owner reviews every Monday — takes 15 minutes. Eliminates the "how much do we have?" phone call from the field.
Effort: 4 hrs to buildImpact: HIGHOwner: Controller / Bookkeeper
3
Automate web-to-lead response in under 5 minutes
Set up a Zapier trigger: new form submission → immediate SMS to prospect + task to estimator with name, number, and job type. Response time drops from 4+ hours to under 2 minutes. Est. close rate improvement: 8–12%.
Effort: 3 hrsImpact: MEDIUM-HIGHOwner: Office Admin
4
Define estimate authority matrix — remove owner from <$15K
Write a one-page authority matrix: estimates under $15K are sent by the estimator without owner review. $15K–$50K requires a 24-hr owner review window. Over $50K, owner reviews before send. Frees ~8 hrs/week of owner time immediately.
Effort: 2 hrsImpact: HIGHOwner: Owner + Estimator
5
Add review request to job completion workflow in ServiceTitan
Trigger a Google Review request SMS automatically when job status moves to "Complete" in ServiceTitan. Target 2 reviews/week. At current close rate, 52 new reviews in 6 months — rating moves from 4.1 to ~4.6.
Effort: 2 hrsImpact: MEDIUMOwner: Dispatcher / Office Admin
