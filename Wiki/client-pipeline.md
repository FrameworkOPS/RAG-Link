**Revenue:** $1M+

**AI adoption:** Zero — no AI tools in use

**Current tech stack:** GoHighLevel (CRM/funnel platform) + Calendly (scheduling)

**Fit assessment:**

Live to Thrive falls outside the primary ICP (home service, $2M–$20M). The coaching space has a different business model — no field technicians, no job costing, no FSM. However, a $1M+ coaching business running GoHighLevel with zero AI adoption has a clear automation opportunity:

- GoHighLevel has native automation capabilities that are almost certainly underutilized
- Zero AI adoption at $1M+ revenue suggests the owner is doing significant manual work that could be automated
- Calendly → GoHighLevel pipeline for lead nurture, onboarding sequences, review requests, and client reactivation are all buildable

**Recommendation for discovery call:** Treat as a potential Automation Build Sprint or AI Readiness Assessment, not a retainer. If the owner is operationally stretched and the business is growing, revisit ICP fit. The coaching business model can absorb a Light retainer ($2,500/mo) if there's enough revenue and the owner is the decision maker.

**Key questions to answer on the call:**
1. What's the primary revenue model — 1:1 coaching, group programs, digital products, or mix?
2. How many clients active at once? What does the service delivery process look like?
3. Where is the owner spending the most time that they shouldn't be?
4. What's the growth goal and timeline?
5. Budget range (ask directly — this is non-optional per the engagement process)

**Pre-call prep:**
- Review GoHighLevel's native automation capabilities (pipeline stages, email/SMS sequences, workflows)
- Know the AI Readiness Assessment product cold — likely the right foot-in-the-door
- Frame the conversation around "what could we automate in the next 30 days that would give you 5 hours back per week" — tangible, not abstract

## Pipeline Qualification Notes

A prospect is worth a discovery call if they have 3+ of these:
- $2M–$20M revenue in home services
- Owner is the decision maker (not a partner committee or board)
- Owner is personally stretched (working 55–70 hrs/week)
- Gross margin declining or unknown
- Software stack they're not getting value from
- Has tried to hire ops help that didn't work out

**Disqualify immediately if:**
- Active turnaround (losing money with no clear path)
- 3+ consultant engagements in 3 years
- Owner won't make decisions without partner/spouse/board approval
- Franchise unit
