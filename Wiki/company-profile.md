# Framework OPS LLC — Company Profile

## Overview
Framework OPS LLC is a fractional COO consulting practice founded by Chance Peare. The firm delivers senior operations leadership to home service companies (and adjacent small/mid-market businesses) on a retainer basis, without the cost of a full-time executive hire.

**Owner**: Chance Peare  
**Email**: chance@frameworkopsllc.com  
**Banking**: Mercury  
**Bookkeeping**: QuickBooks Online (QBO)  
**Founded**: 2025–2026 (early stage, actively building)

## Mission
Bring Fortune 500-level operational discipline — with an AI-forward edge — to home service companies that are scaling but don't have the internal infrastructure to support that growth.

## Ideal Client Profile (ICP)
**Primary**: Home service companies
- Roofing, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, landscaping, pest control, restoration
- Revenue range: $3M–$50M
- Typically founder-led, hitting operational ceiling
- Pain points: inconsistent processes, poor tech stack, manual workflows, no data visibility, team accountability gaps

**Secondary**: Small/mid-market businesses in adjacent industries (coaching, professional services, acquisitions) where operational chaos is limiting growth.

## Differentiation
1. **AI-first operator**: Every engagement includes automation and tech stack leverage — not just process documentation but actual implementation
2. **Home service depth**: Current COO role at a large roofing company provides live, current-market credibility
3. **Results-tied engagement**: Fractional model means Chance's incentive is ROI, not hours billed
4. **Speed to impact**: 10+ years of organizational leadership compressed into a playbook that executes fast
