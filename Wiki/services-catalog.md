# Framework OPS Services Catalog

## Pricing Philosophy

Retainer prices are fixed, not negotiable. Sprints carry ranges to account for scope variance at intake. The service menu is structured in three groups: Entry / Trust Builders, Productized Sprints, and Retainers.

The engagement always starts with an OPS Assessment or diagnostic before a retainer is proposed. No proposal without a completed diagnostic. No kickoff without signed agreement AND first month's payment received.

**Do not discount to close.** Scope down to a lower tier or a Sprint instead. Discounted retainers signal low confidence and attract clients who undervalue the work.

---

## Group 1 — Entry / Trust Builders

Low-risk, defined deliverables. Designed to generate trust and demonstrate value before a longer commitment. Primary gateway to retainer pipeline.

### AI Readiness Assessment — $297

**Deliverable:** Written assessment of where the client currently stands on AI and automation adoption, plus a prioritized list of 5–8 automation opportunities specific to their operation.

**Format:** Async. Client completes structured intake form. Chance delivers a 2–3 page written report within 5 business days. One 30-minute review call included.

**Best for:** Owners curious about AI/automation but don't know where to start.

**Upsell path:** Automation Sprint or retainer.

---

### Tech Stack Audit — $497

**Deliverable:** Documented audit of the client's current software stack (FSM, accounting, communication, HR, automation). What they have, how it's being used vs. how it should be used, what's redundant, what's missing, and a prioritized action list.

**Format:** Client grants view access to current tools + completes intake form. Chance delivers a 3–4 page written audit report + 45-minute debrief call.

**Best for:** Companies that have bought software but aren't sure if they're getting full value, or companies about to make a new technology investment.

**Upsell path:** Tech stack implementation support, Automation Sprint, or retainer.

---

### OPS Assessment — $997 ⭐ PRIMARY GATEWAY PRODUCT

**Deliverable:** Comprehensive operational diagnostic covering finance, operations, people, and technology. Includes a prioritized problem matrix, quick win identification, and a recommended roadmap for the next 90 days.

**Format:** 90-minute video diagnostic session + document review (P&L, FSM walkthrough, org overview) + written diagnostic report (4–6 pages) delivered within 5 business days + 60-minute debrief and roadmap call.

**Critical note:** If the client proceeds to a retainer within 30 days of the OPS Assessment, the $997 fee is credited toward the first month's retainer. This makes it low-risk for the client and creates a natural urgency for retainer conversion.

**Best for:** Any home service operator who wants to understand where their business actually stands operationally. The highest-value entry product.

**Upsell path:** Productized Sprint or retainer at any tier.

## Group 2 — Productized Sprints

Fixed scope, fixed price, defined timeline. For clients with a specific operational gap to close who aren't ready for or don't need a full retainer.

### SOP Sprint — $2,000

**Deliverable:** 5 fully documented standard operating procedures for the client's highest-priority processes. Includes process mapping, written SOP in the Framework OPS template, training guide for each SOP, and recommended storage/delivery method.

**Timeline:** 3–4 weeks

**Client requirement:** Two 60-minute working sessions + access to the team members who currently own each process.

**Typical SOPs covered:** Dispatch/scheduling, job closeout, estimate process, customer complaint handling, new employee onboarding.

---

### Automation Build Sprint — $2,000

**Deliverable:** Design, build, test, and deploy 2–3 automation workflows for the client's operation. Includes documentation of each automation so the client's team can maintain it.

**Timeline:** 3–4 weeks

**Common automations built:** Lead follow-up (web form → SMS/email), appointment confirmation and reminders, review request post-job completion, invoice follow-up sequence, weekly reporting pull.

**Tools:** Zapier, Make, n8n, or native FSM automations depending on the client's stack.

---

### KPI Dashboard Build — $2,000

**Deliverable:** A live KPI dashboard built on the client's existing tools — revenue, gross margin, close rate, job costing, and cash position visible in one place. Includes a weekly auto-report delivered to the owner's inbox every Monday morning.

**Timeline:** 2–3 weeks

**Format:** One 60-minute scoping call → build and test → 45-minute handoff call. Final output: live dashboard + automated weekly report running in the client's stack.

**Best for:** Operators flying blind on their numbers or spending hours each week manually compiling reports.

---

### 90-Day Roadmap Sprint — $2,000–$3,500

**Deliverable:** A structured 90-day operational roadmap tailored to the client's specific pain profile. Includes prioritized initiative list, owner accountability framework, and milestone check-ins.

**Timeline:** 2–4 weeks to build; 90-day execution roadmap delivered.

### Advisory — $1,500/month

**Hours:** ~4–6/month | **Min term:** 3 months

Senior sounding board only. Two monthly calls, async review of documents and reports. No implementation. Best for companies with a capable internal operator who just needs strategic guidance.

**Realistic expectation:** This tier does not transform operations. It helps owners make fewer bad decisions. Do not position it as transformation work.

---

### Light — $2,500/month

**Hours:** ~8–12/month | **Min term:** 3 months

Weekly working sessions with some implementation support. Builds the KPI dashboard and cash flow model, develops one SOP per month, provides same-day async support. Good fit for $2M–$4M companies beginning to put systems in place.

---

### Active — $4,000/month (Most Common)

**Hours:** ~15–20/month | **Min term:** 6 months

The core engagement. Weekly sessions, full financial visibility stack (cash flow model + job costing), 2–3 SOPs per month, automation build each quarter, same-day async support.

At $4,000/month, the client needs to see a clear path to $12,000+ in measurable value creation within 90 days. Realistic value drivers: gross margin improvement from job costing visibility, reduced owner hours from delegation systems, cash flow clarity preventing costly borrowing decisions.

**Competitive benchmark:** An operations manager costs $55K–$80K/year ($4,600–$6,700/month) plus benefits, hiring risk, and management overhead. The Active retainer delivers senior COO-level work at $4,000/month with none of those costs.

---

### Embedded — $6,000/month

**Hours:** ~30–40/month | **Min term:** 6–12 months

Deep integration into the client's leadership structure. Two weekly sessions, direct manager coaching, full SOP library buildout, monthly automation builds, quarterly on-site visits, hiring process ownership.

**Best for:** $8M–$20M companies in active growth events — new market entry, leadership transition, PE prep.

## Engagement Process Summary

Every engagement follows this sequence regardless of tier. Total contact-to-kickoff: 2–4 weeks.

1. **Discovery Call (30–45 min)** — mutual qualification, revenue/pain/budget discussed directly
2. **Pre-Diagnostic Intake (written form)** — client spends 60–90 min answering structured questions before the diagnostic
3. **Operational Diagnostic (90 min + doc review)** — P&L, FSM walkthrough, org overview; outputs a priority matrix and recommended tier
4. **Proposal (4–6 pages, PDF)** — reviewed live on 30-min call; 7-day decision window
5. **Agreement + Kickoff** — signed agreement + first month's payment before kickoff is scheduled
6. **Ongoing:** 30-day diagnostic phase → 60-day quick wins → 90-day review

**Hard rules:**
- No proposal without completed diagnostic
- No kickoff without signed agreement AND payment received
- No open-ended proposals (7-day expiration)
- One proposed tier per proposal — diagnose, then propose one, not a menu
