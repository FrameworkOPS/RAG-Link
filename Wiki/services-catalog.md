# Framework OPS — Services Catalog

## Engagement Model
All engagements are monthly retainers. No hourly billing. Scope is defined upfront; Chance operates as a fractional member of the client's leadership team.

## Service Tiers

### Tier 1 — Fractional COO Retainer (Core)
**Price**: $1,500–$8,000/month depending on scope and hours  
**Typical hours**: 10–40 hrs/month  
**Deliverables**:
- Weekly/bi-weekly EOS L10 leadership meetings (facilitated)
- Monthly scorecard + KPI reporting
- Process documentation (SOPs for key workflows)
- Tech stack audit and optimization recommendations
- Direct Slack/email access for operational questions
- Quarterly rocks and 90-day planning facilitation

### Tier 2 — AI & Automation Implementation
**Add-on or standalone**: $1,500–$3,000/month  
**Deliverables**:
- Workflow automation (Zapier, Make, n8n, or custom)
- AI tool selection and deployment (Claude, GPT-based tools, industry-specific)
- CRM/field management software optimization
- Reporting dashboards (QBO integration, ops data)
- RAG knowledge base setup for internal AI assistants

### Tier 3 — Project-Based (SOW)
**Price**: Custom  
**Use cases**:
- Tech stack migration or implementation
- M&A operational due diligence and integration
- Team restructuring / org design
- Hiring process buildout (scorecards, interview frameworks)

## EOS/L10 Meeting Framework
Chance runs EOS (Entrepreneurial Operating System) L10 meetings for clients:
- **Weekly L10**: 90 min — scorecard review, rocks update, to-do list, issues (IDS)
- **Quarterly planning**: Half-day — set 90-day rocks, review annual goals
- **Annual planning**: Full day — vision/traction organizer, 1-year + 3-year targets

## Pricing Philosophy
- Pessimistic on timeline-to-value; always under-promise
- Retainer pricing reflects senior leadership, not consulting rates
- No long-term contracts — month-to-month after initial 3-month commitment
- Referral network: Carlos/TeamOS acquisition targets as secondary pipeline
