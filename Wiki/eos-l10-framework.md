# EOS / L10 Meeting Framework

## What is EOS?
EOS (Entrepreneurial Operating System) is a complete business operating framework designed for small/mid-market companies. Framework OPS uses EOS as the primary operating model for client engagements.

Key components:
- **Vision/Traction Organizer (V/TO)**: 10-year target, 3-year picture, 1-year plan
- **Rocks**: 90-day priorities (3–7 per person/team)
- **Scorecard**: Weekly measurables with owner and target
- **Issues List**: Captured and solved through IDS (Identify, Discuss, Solve)
- **Meeting Pulse**: Weekly L10s + quarterly and annual planning

## L10 Meeting Structure (90 minutes)
1. **Segue** (5 min) — Personal best / business best to open
2. **Scorecard** (5 min) — Review weekly measurables; identify off-track items
3. **Rock Review** (5 min) — On track / off track for 90-day rocks
4. **Customer/Employee Headlines** (5 min) — Good news and bad news
5. **To-Do List** (5 min) — Review prior week's 7-day actions (100% completion expected)
6. **IDS** (60 min) — Issues list: prioritize top 3, solve each to completion
7. **Conclude** (5 min) — Recap new to-dos, rating (1–10), cascading messages

## How Chance Runs L10s
- Facilitates as the COO/integrator role
- Keeps the meeting on time — hard stop on segue/scorecard/rocks
- Captures issues in real time (ClickUp or shared doc)
- Pushes teams to solve issues at the root, not symptoms
- Follows up on to-dos as accountability owner

## Quarterly Planning
- Half-day session (3–4 hours)
- Review: Did we hit last quarter's rocks?
- Set: 3–7 rocks for next quarter per person
- Prioritize: Company rocks first, then department/individual
- Output: Updated V/TO with 1-year goals refreshed

## Annual Planning
- Full-day session
- Review 3-year picture vs reality
- Reset 1-year plan
- Identify people/process/platform gaps
- Output: Full V/TO refresh + next year's rocks

## Common Issues in Home Service L10s
- Scorecard measurables often undefined at start → Chance's first task is building a scorecard
- Issues list gets long and doesn't get solved → IDS discipline is key
- Rocks get set but not resourced → Connect rocks to actual weekly to-dos
- Leadership team accountability is weak → Peer pressure from scorecard visibility
