# Client Profile: Carlos / TeamOS

## Overview

Carlos is Framework OPS's second active client and a structurally distinct engagement from Skyright. Where Skyright is a single large operating company, Carlos (TeamOS) is a serial acquirer running multiple home service businesses through a roll-up model. The engagement focuses on acquisition-side operational readiness and ongoing operational infrastructure for the portfolio companies.

## Contact Information

- **Client name:** Carlos
- **Email:** carlos@crpcfoservices.com
- **Business:** TeamOS

## Engagement Details

- **Monthly retainer:** $3,000/month
- **Retainer tier:** Light-to-Active (scope and time reflect Light tier pricing with Active-level strategic engagement given complexity of the acquisition model)
- **Engagement type:** Ongoing fractional COO / acquisition operations

## Business Model

Carlos is a serial acquirer in the home service space, running acquisition roll-ups:

- **First roll-up:** ~$80M in acquired businesses
- **Second roll-up:** ~$40M in acquired businesses
- **Current pace:** 1–3 deals per year
- **Growth trajectory:** Active — portfolio growing; acquisitions continuing

The roll-up model means Carlos is not running a single operational company — he is managing a portfolio of acquired home service businesses, each at different stages of integration and operational maturity. The COO challenge is fundamentally different from a standalone operator: systems need to be portable across acquisitions, integration timelines need to be predictable, and each new acquisition adds operational complexity to the portfolio.

## What Chance Does for Carlos / TeamOS

### Acquisition-Side Operational Diligence

When Carlos is evaluating a new acquisition, Chance supports operational due diligence:
- Assessing the target company's operational infrastructure (SOPs, FSM setup, job costing, org chart)
- Identifying integration complexity and time-to-profitability risks
- Estimating operational improvement runway post-acquisition
- Flagging key person dependencies that create integration risk

### Portfolio Operations Management

Across the portfolio:
- Building repeatable operational playbooks that can be applied to each new acquisition
- Standardizing KPI reporting so Carlos has visibility across all portfolio companies in a consistent format
- Cash flow monitoring across portfolio companies — particularly important when multiple acquisitions are in flight simultaneously
- Tech stack standardization: identifying which FSM and accounting tools should be the portfolio standard, and managing migrations

### Ongoing Cadence

- Weekly or bi-weekly working sessions with Carlos
- Async support for acquisition-related questions, deal evaluation, and operational issues in portfolio companies
- Framework OPS serves as the operational brain trust that Carlos can pull on without hiring a full-time COO at each portfolio company

## Operational Considerations for a Roll-Up Model

Home service roll-ups have specific operational challenges Chance addresses:

### Integration Failure Modes
- **Key person flight:** Acquired companies often lose their best operator (who becomes newly wealthy and exits) within 12–18 months. Systems must be documented before the acquisition closes, not after.
- **Culture merge failure:** Acquired companies have their own dispatch culture, pricing norms, and customer relationships. Forcing the acquirer's system too fast kills morale and customers.
- **Tech stack fragmentation:** Multiple acquired companies each running different FSMs, different QBO chart-of-accounts structures, different payroll systems. The integration cost is enormous if not planned.
- **Reporting black holes:** Carlos can't manage a portfolio he can't see. Standardized weekly reporting across all portfolio companies is the highest-priority infrastructure build.

### What a Portable Operational Playbook Includes
- Standard chart of accounts (QBO) across all portfolio companies
- Uniform KPI definitions (gross margin, DSO, close rate, callback rate) so apples-to-apples comparison is possible
- Integration checklist: 90-day post-acquisition operational transition steps
- FSM preference (if possible, standardize on one) with migration support
- Job costing methodology that applies regardless of trade vertical
