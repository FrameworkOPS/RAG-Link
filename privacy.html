Privacy Policy

Framework OPS LLC · Effective May 18, 2026 · Last updated May 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Framework OPS LLC, a Florida limited liability company (“Framework OPS,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use our software application that integrates with Intuit QuickBooks Online (the “Application”) and our related websites and services (collectively, the “Services”).

1. Who This Applies To

This Policy applies to customers, prospective customers, and authorized users of businesses that connect the Application to their Intuit QuickBooks Online (“QBO”) company file. If you are a customer of one of our business clients, the business that engages us is the controller of its own data; this Policy describes our practices as a service provider/processor.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide

Account details (name, email, company name), authentication tokens, support and onboarding correspondence, and any information you submit when contacting us.

2.2 Information from Intuit QuickBooks Online

When you connect the Application to your QBO company file via OAuth, we access financial and business information through the Intuit API. Depending on what you ask the Application to do, this can include:

  • Company profile and settings;
  • Chart of accounts;
  • Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet, and other financial reports;
  • Accounts receivable and accounts payable detail and aging;
  • Customers, vendors, and contact records;
  • Products and services / catalog data;
  • Invoices, estimates, payments, and payment links;
  • Transaction-level records associated with the above.

The Application is also authorized to create and update certain records on your behalf, including invoices, estimates, payment links, and customer records.

2.3 Information collected automatically

Log data (IP address, user agent, timestamps, request paths), error and diagnostic data, and basic usage metrics necessary to operate and secure the Services.

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the Application and Services;
  • To execute the specific actions you direct (e.g., generate a report, create an invoice);
  • To provide AI-assisted analysis, summaries, and recommendations using Customer Data you submit (see Section 4 below);
  • To authenticate users, secure the Services, and prevent fraud or abuse;
  • To provide customer support and respond to inquiries;
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our agreements.

We do not sell your personal information or your QBO financial data. We do not use Customer Data to train third-party AI models, and our agreements with AI providers prohibit such use of your data.

5. Data Retention

We retain Customer Data for as long as your connection to the Application is active and for a limited period thereafter as needed to provide the Services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. You may request deletion as described in Section 8. OAuth tokens are revoked promptly upon disconnection.

6. Security

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect information, including encryption of data in transit (TLS), encryption at rest for credentials and tokens, access controls, audit logging, and least-privilege principles for staff access. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. International Data

The Services are operated from the United States. If you access them from outside the United States, your information may be transferred to and processed in the United States and other jurisdictions where our service providers operate.

8. Your Choices and Rights

  • Disconnect: You may revoke the Application’s access to your QBO company file at any time from within Intuit QuickBooks Online (My Apps).
  • Access, correction, deletion: You may request a copy of personal information we hold about you, request corrections, or request deletion, subject to applicable law. To make a request, email chance@frameworkopsllc.com.
  • State privacy rights: Residents of California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, and other states with applicable privacy laws may have additional rights, including the right to opt out of certain uses. We do not sell personal information and do not engage in cross-context behavioral advertising.

9. Children’s Privacy

The Services are not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Intuit-Specific Disclosures

Framework OPS’ use and transfer of information received from Intuit APIs adheres to the Intuit Developer Terms, including any limited-use requirements. We access only the scopes you authorize, use the data only to provide the features you request, and do not transfer the data except as described in this Policy.
