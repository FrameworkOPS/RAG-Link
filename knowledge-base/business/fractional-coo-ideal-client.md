# Ideal Client Profile (ICP) — Framework OPS LLC

## Overview

The ICP for Framework OPS is a founder-led home service company in the $2M–$20M revenue range that is experiencing the pain of scaling without the operational infrastructure to support it. The owner is the best salesperson, the best technician, or both — and the business is constrained by their personal bandwidth.

Not every home service company in this revenue range is a fit. The criteria below separate buyers from prospects.

---

## Company Profile

### Revenue Range
**$2M–$20M annual revenue** is the target band.

- **Under $2M:** Unit economics don't support the investment. At $1.5M revenue with 30% gross margin ($450K gross profit) and $350K overhead, there's $100K in net income. A $4,500/month retainer is 54% of that — too large a share for the owner to stomach even if the ROI is real. These companies need a business coach or an entry-level ops hire, not a fractional COO.

- **$2M–$8M (Sweet Spot):** This is where the highest pain and highest ROI overlap. Company is too big to operate informally but doesn't have the revenue base to justify a $150K+ COO. The owner is stretched. There is enough gross profit to fund the engagement and measure results. This is 80% of Framework OPS pipeline.

- **$8M–$20M:** Viable engagements but require Tier 3 scope. Usually has some internal management layer already. The fractional COO role becomes more "upgrade the operating system" than "build from scratch." Higher complexity, higher fee.

- **Over $20M:** Typically has or needs a full-time COO. Fractional doesn't make sense unless it's a transition or very specific project.

### Industries
Primary: Roofing, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, landscaping/lawn care, pest control, painting, garage door, pool service

Secondary: Any residential or commercial home service with job-based revenue model and field technicians

Not a fit: Remodeling/GC (different business model), pure retail, manufacturing

### Company Structure
- Founder or owner-operator is still active in the business (not absentee ownership)
- 5–75 employees
- Has at least one full-time office staff member (someone to implement changes with)
- NOT a franchise (franchise ops are dictated by franchisor — limited COO intervention surface)

---

## Owner / Decision Maker Profile

The decision maker is almost always the owner/founder. In rare cases it's a partnership or a private equity-backed operator.

### Demographics
- Age 32–55
- Has built the business largely through hustle, relationships, and grit
- BBA or no college (less common: MBA or trades background)
- Does not consider themselves "a systems person"
- Has seen the business grow faster than the operations can handle

### Pain Profile (What They're Experiencing)
- Works 55–70 hours/week and can't figure out how to get less in the weeds
- Has tried to hire an office manager or ops manager to fix things — didn't work the way they hoped
- Has bought software (ServiceTitan, etc.) that the team isn't using well
- Gross margin is declining as the company grows (a telling signal of systems failure)
- Has a key person dependency problem: one critical employee holds everything together
- Losing sleep over payroll, or has a "good problem" of fast growth without the infrastructure to support it

### Motivations
- "I want to grow to $X without it killing me"
- "I want to take a real vacation"
- "I want to sell this business in 3–5 years and need it to run without me"
- "My key person is leaving and I need systems before they go"

### What They Are NOT Looking For
- Another consultant who writes a report and disappears
- Someone who tells them what they already know
- A generalist who "does strategy" but can't advise on ServiceTitan or job costing
- Being told their business is broken — they know. They want it fixed.

---

## Organizational Symptoms (Buying Triggers)

Any 3 of these present = high-likelihood fit:

- [ ] Owner is approving every discount or estimate personally
- [ ] Gross margin declining year-over-year despite revenue growth
- [ ] Same problems recurring month after month with no resolution
- [ ] A recent "near miss" on payroll or a cash flow crisis
- [ ] A key person departure that left the company exposed
- [ ] Software purchased 6+ months ago that isn't being used properly
- [ ] Owner mentions they haven't taken a real vacation in 2+ years
- [ ] Company crossed a growth milestone ($3M, $5M, $10M) and operations haven't caught up
- [ ] Owner recently tried to hire a COO/ops manager and it didn't work out

---

## Disqualifiers (Do Not Pursue)

- Owner is not the primary decision maker (spouse, partner, board must all agree — too slow)
- Company has experienced 3+ significant consultant engagements in 3 years (consultant dependency, implementation resistance)
- Owner wants results without accountability or implementation effort
- Primary issue is culture/values or an owner in active personal crisis — not an ops problem
- Company is actively losing money with no clear path to profitability — this is a turnaround, not an ops upgrade
- Owner's ego is tied to "doing it my way" — will not implement recommendations

## Target Acquisition Channels

1. **LinkedIn** — Direct outreach to owners in home service space, organic content positioning
2. **Referral network** — Roofing suppliers, HVAC distributors, commercial insurance brokers, accountants (QBO-connected CPAs), business bankers (Mercury, Live Oak)
3. **Industry associations** — NRCA (roofing), ACCA (HVAC), PHCC (plumbing/HVAC), NALP (landscaping)
4. **Speaking/content** — Podcasts, trade publications, local chamber events
5. **Cold outreach** — LinkedIn DMs to verified owners in target size range + industry
