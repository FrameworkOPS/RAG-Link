# Framework OPS Service Tiers

## Pricing Philosophy

Pricing reflects time, expertise, and implementation risk — not what the market will theoretically bear. Retainer prices are fixed, not ranges. Sprints are flat-priced at $2,000 each — no ranges. Scope is held to the fixed deliverable; if a prospect needs more, scope up to a retainer rather than inflating the sprint price.

The service menu is structured in three groups: **Entry / Trust Builders** (low-cost, defined-scope entry points), **Productized Sprints** (fixed-scope project work), and **Retainers** (ongoing fractional COO engagements). A prospect doesn't have to start with a retainer — the entry products are designed to generate trust and demonstrate value before a longer commitment.

## Group 1 — Entry / Trust Builders

Low-risk, defined deliverables. These exist to get a foot in the door with operators who aren't ready to commit to a retainer. They also generate referrals and surface retainer candidates.

### AI Readiness Assessment — $297

**Deliverable:** A written assessment of where the client's home service business currently stands on AI and automation adoption, plus a prioritized list of 5–8 automation opportunities specific to their operation.

**Format:** Async. Client completes a structured intake form. Chance reviews and delivers a 2–3 page written report within 5 business days. One 30-minute review call included.

**Best for:** Owners curious about AI/automation but don't know where to start. Low-friction first engagement.

**Upsell path:** Automation Sprint or retainer.

---

### Tech Stack Audit — $497

**Deliverable:** A documented audit of the client's current software stack (FSM, accounting, communication, HR, automation tools). Includes: what they have, how it's being used vs. how it should be used, what's redundant, what's missing, and a prioritized action list.

**Format:** Client grants view access to current tools + completes intake form. Chance delivers a 3–4 page written audit report + 45-minute debrief call.

**Best for:** Companies that have bought software but aren't sure if they're getting full value from it, or companies about to make a new technology investment.

**Upsell path:** Tech stack implementation support, Automation Sprint, or retainer.

---

### OPS Assessment — $997 ⭐ START HERE

**Deliverable:** A comprehensive operational diagnostic covering finance, operations, people, and technology. Includes a prioritized problem matrix, quick win identification, and a recommended roadmap for the next 90 days.

**Format:** 90-minute video diagnostic session + document review (P&L, FSM walkthrough, org overview) + written diagnostic report (4–6 pages) delivered within 5 business days + 60-minute debrief and roadmap call.

**Best for:** Any home service operator who wants to understand where their business actually stands operationally. This is the highest-value entry product and the primary gateway to a retainer engagement.

**Note:** If the client proceeds to a retainer within 30 days of the OPS Assessment, the $997 fee is credited toward the first month's retainer.

**Upsell path:** Productized Sprint or retainer at any tier.

## Group 2 — Productized Sprints

Fixed scope, fixed price, defined timeline. Sprints are project engagements — not ongoing. They are designed for clients who have a specific operational gap to close but aren't ready for or don't need a full retainer.

### SOP Sprint — $2,000

**Deliverable:** 5 fully documented standard operating procedures for the client's highest-priority processes. Includes process mapping, written SOP in the Framework OPS template, training guide for each SOP, and a recommended storage/delivery method for the team.

**Timeline:** 3–4 weeks

**Client requirement:** Two 60-minute working sessions + access to the team members who currently own each process.

**Typical SOPs covered:** Dispatch/scheduling, job closeout, estimate process, customer complaint handling, new employee onboarding (client selects 5).

---

### Automation Build Sprint — $2,000

**Deliverable:** Design, build, test, and deploy 2–3 automation workflows for the client's operation. Includes documentation of each automation so the client's team can maintain it.

**Timeline:** 3–4 weeks

**Common automations built:** Lead follow-up (web form → SMS/email), appointment confirmation and reminders, review request post-job completion, invoice follow-up sequence, weekly reporting pull.

**Tools:** Zapier, Make, n8n, or native FSM automations depending on the client's stack and technical comfort level.

**Client requirement:** Software access, one 60-minute scoping call, and a designated point of contact for testing.

---

### KPI Dashboard Build — $2,000

**Deliverable:** A live KPI dashboard built on the client's existing tools — revenue, gross margin, close rate, job costing, and cash position visible in one place. Includes a weekly auto-report delivered to the owner's inbox every Monday morning. No more pulling numbers manually.

**Timeline:** 2–3 weeks

**Format:** One 60-minute scoping call to confirm data sources and key metrics → build and test → 45-minute handoff call with the owner and whoever manages reporting. Final output: live dashboard + automated weekly report running in the client's stack.

**Best for:** Operators who are flying blind on their numbers or spending hours each week manually compiling reports. Creates a durable asset that pulls toward a retainer — owner sees the data, wants someone to act on it.

### Advisory
**$1,500/month | 3-month minimum | ~4–6 hours/month**

Senior sounding board only. Two monthly calls, async review of documents and reports. No implementation. Best for companies with a capable internal operator who just needs strategic guidance.

Realistic expectation: this tier does not transform operations. It helps owners make fewer bad decisions. Do not position it as transformation work.

---

### Light
**$2,500/month | 3-month minimum | ~8–12 hours/month**

Weekly working sessions with some implementation support. Builds the KPI dashboard and cash flow model, develops one SOP per month, provides same-day async support. Good fit for $2M–$4M companies beginning to put systems in place.

---

### Active (Most Common)
**$4,000/month | 6-month minimum | ~15–20 hours/month**

The core engagement. Weekly sessions, full financial visibility stack (cash flow model + job costing), 2–3 SOPs per month, automation build each quarter, same-day support. This is where Framework OPS delivers the highest ROI for the $3M–$10M home service operator.

At $4,000/month, the client needs to see a clear path to $12,000+ in measurable value creation within 90 days. Realistic value drivers: gross margin improvement from job costing visibility, reduced owner hours from delegation systems, cash flow clarity preventing costly borrowing decisions.

---

### Embedded
**$6,000/month | 6–12 month minimum | ~30–40 hours/month**

Deep integration into the client's leadership structure. Two weekly sessions, direct manager coaching, full SOP library, monthly automation builds, quarterly on-site visits, hiring process ownership. Best for $8M–$20M companies in active growth events: new market, leadership transition, PE prep.

## Pricing Anchors

Most operators in the $3M–$10M range benchmark a fractional COO against:
- A full-time ops manager ($55K–$80K salary = $4,600–$6,700/month all-in, plus hiring risk)
- A business coach ($1,000–$3,000/month, no implementation)

Framework OPS sits above coaching (delivers implementation, not just advice) and below a full-time hire (no benefits, no severance, fractional cost, senior expertise). The Active tier is the competitive sweet spot for initial client acquisition.

**Do not discount to close.** Scope-down to a lower tier or a Sprint instead. Discounted retainers signal low confidence and attract clients who undervalue the work.
