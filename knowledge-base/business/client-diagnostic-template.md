# Client Diagnostic Template

## How to Use This Template

This template is designed for the 90-minute operational diagnostic session conducted after the discovery call and pre-intake form. It is a conversation guide, not a rigid script. Follow the thread that surfaces the real problem — often it's not the problem the owner described in the discovery call.

Take notes directly in this document. The completed diagnostic becomes the basis for the proposal.

**Pre-Session Checklist:**
- [ ] Pre-intake form received and reviewed
- [ ] Last 12 months P&L requested (ideally received before this call)
- [ ] Screen share ready for FSM walkthrough
- [ ] Recording enabled (with owner permission)

---

## Part 1: Business Overview

### Revenue and Growth
- What was your revenue last year? The year before?
- What's your projected revenue this year?
- Where is revenue coming from? (residential vs. commercial, by service line)
- Is the business growing, flat, or declining? What's driving it?

### Service Mix
- What services do you offer? Which generates the most revenue? Which generates the most profit (or do you know)?
- Any services you're adding, dropping, or considering?
- Seasonality: what do your high and low months look like? What does cash look like in the slow months?

### Geographic Footprint
- Single market or multiple?
- Considering expansion?

## Part 2: Operations

### Scheduling and Dispatch
- Walk me through what happens when a new job gets booked. Who does what?
- How is the schedule managed day-to-day? (Software, whiteboard, calls?)
- What happens when a job gets cancelled last minute?
- What are your biggest scheduling headaches right now?

### Field Operations
- How many crews/technicians do you have in the field?
- How do you measure field productivity? (Jobs per day, revenue per tech?)
- What does a job closeout look like? Who does what when the job is done?
- How do you handle quality control? Who catches mistakes?
- What is your callback or warranty call rate? What's causing most of them?

### Technology
- What software are you using to run the business? (FSM, CRM, accounting)
- How long have you been on it? How would you rate adoption?
- What are you NOT doing in the software that you should be?
- Are there things being done manually that you know should be automated?

**Red flags to probe:** Using software they bought but aren't using. Running operations from spreadsheets. No job costing in the FSM. Manual AR collections.

## Part 3: Finance

### P&L Review (conduct live with shared screen or document)
- What is your gross margin percentage? Does it vary by service line?
- What are your top 3 overhead expense categories?
- How has profit margin trended over the last 2–3 years?
- Do you know your cost per job? Cost per lead?

### Cash Flow
- How do you manage cash flow today?
- Have you ever missed payroll or had a close call?
- What does your AR aging look like? Do you have slow-paying customers?
- Do you have a line of credit? When do you typically use it?
- Do you have a cash reserve? How many weeks of runway?

### Pricing and Job Costing
- How do you price jobs? (Flat rate, T&M, estimate, gut feel?)
- Do you track actual vs. estimated cost per job?
- Are you confident your prices cover your costs and generate margin? How do you know?

## Part 4: People and Organization

### Org Structure
- Who does what? Walk me through who is in what role.
- Who do you rely on most? What happens if they left tomorrow?
- Who reports to you directly? How much time does that take each week?

### Management
- Do you have any managers or leads between you and the field?
- How do you hold people accountable? Do you have regular 1:1s or team meetings?
- What is your leadership team's biggest weakness?

### Hiring and Turnover
- What has your turnover been like in the last year? Why are people leaving?
- How do you find and hire new employees? What does the process look like?
- What is your current biggest hiring challenge?

### Compensation
- How are field employees paid? (Hourly, flat rate, commission?)
- Are there performance bonuses? How are they structured?
- Are you confident your compensation structure is competitive?

## Part 5: Technology and Systems

### Documented Processes
- Do you have written SOPs for any processes? How many?
- What would happen if your best office person quit and someone new had to step in?
- Where does tribal knowledge live right now?

### Reporting and KPIs
- What numbers do you look at regularly? How often?
- Do you have a dashboard or scorecard? How is it built?
- What KPIs do you wish you had that you don't currently track?

## Part 6: Goals and Pain

### 12-Month Goals
- If we're talking in 12 months and it was a great year, what happened?
- What specific revenue, margin, or personal goals are you working toward?
- What is the single biggest constraint on getting there?

### Pain and Urgency
- What keeps you up at night about the business?
- Why now — what triggered you to reach out at this point vs. 6 months ago?
- Have you worked with any consultants or coaches before? What happened?

### Owner's Role
- What does your week look like? Where do you spend most of your time?
- What would you stop doing tomorrow if someone could take it off your plate?
- What would 10 hours/week back mean for you personally and for the business?
