## 1. Executive Summary

Framework OPS LLC is a fractional COO consulting practice serving home service companies in the $2M–$20M revenue band (roofing, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, landscaping, pest, painting, garage door, pool). The practice is differentiated by lived operator experience (Chance is a sitting COO at a large roofing company) and aggressive use of AI/automation in delivery.

The 3-year plan is to build from a $98K Year 1 revenue base (alongside Skyright W2 income) to ~$195K standalone revenue by Year 3, with controlled growth through a tested service menu, a defined ICP, and a deliberate hiring sequence (solo → VA → US associate) that follows revenue, not the other way around.

The biggest risk is Year 2: Skyright W2 ($96K stable income) ends, replaced by uncontracted retainer revenue subject to industry-standard 30%+ annual churn. Conservative take-home Y2 lands $55-65K below current Y1 levels. Plan is built around this transition with explicit pipeline milestones and emergency cash buffer.

## 2. Company Description

### Mission
Help home service companies in the $2M–$20M range install the operational systems, KPIs, and automation that owner-operators don't have time to build themselves — fractional COO leverage without the full-time hire.

### Why now
The home service trades have specific structural characteristics — high transaction volume, recurring service models, owner-operators bottlenecked at every decision — that map directly to operational systems work. AI and modern tooling (Make, Zapier, ServiceTitan APIs, automated job costing) have made it possible to install $250K+ of value with a fractional engagement, where five years ago this required a full-time hire or a six-figure consulting firm engagement.

### Founder credentials
- Sitting COO at large roofing company (active operator, not "ex-")
- BBA, MBA
- 10+ years organizational leadership
- Banking: Mercury. Books: QBO. Vault: Obsidian on `knowledge-base/`.

### Entity structure
- Single-member LLC, FL (Tarpon Springs)
- Default tax treatment: disregarded entity (Schedule C). NOT electing S-corp until 1099 income clears ~$100K consistently
- Liability separation between Framework OPS and Chance personally is a core reason to bill through the LLC even with no current tax benefit

### Differentiator vs other fractional COOs
Most fractional COOs come from corporate or SaaS backgrounds and have to learn home service vocabulary, math, and culture. Framework OPS has lived it. The wedge is credibility, not methodology.

## 3. Market & Ideal Client Profile

### Target market
Home service trade companies in the United States:
- Revenue $2M–$20M (sweet spot $2M–$8M = 80% of pipeline)
- Owner-operator still active in the business
- 5–75 employees, minimum 1 office staffer to implement with
- Trades only — NOT remodeling/GC, retail, manufacturing, franchise units

### Buying triggers (need 3+ for fit)
- Declining gross margin
- Owner approves every estimate
- Recent near-miss on payroll
- Key-person departure
- Multiple expensive software tools unused
- No vacation in 2+ years
- Recent failed COO/ops hire

### Disqualifiers
- Pre-revenue or sub-$1M
- Consultant-carousel history (>3 prior consultants in 5 years)
- Owner unwilling to implement
- Active financial turnaround (losing money currently)
- Ego-locked owner who won't take direction

### Market sizing (rough, not basis for revenue)
US home service market is fragmented — ~250K independent contractors and small businesses in target trades. Realistically addressable for fractional COO at $2-20M revenue: ~25-35K companies nationally. Sub-vertical concentration is the strategic move (roofing first, given founder background) rather than horizontal coverage.

## 6. Sales & Marketing Plan

### Pipeline funnel (pessimistic — see `income_forecaster.xlsx` Pipeline & Capacity tab)

**Cold path:**
| Stage | Conversion | Volume per close |
|---|---|---|
| Cold contacts | top of funnel | 200 |
| Replies | 10% | 20 |
| Discovery calls | 35% of replies | 7 |
| Paid diagnostics ($997) | 55% of discovery | ~4 |
| Proposals | 75% of diagnostics | ~3 |
| Signed retainer | 35% close | 1 |

**Warm/referral path:** ~3x more efficient. 60 warm intros → 1 close. ~50 hrs of sales work vs ~75 hrs cold.

### Annual sales time required

| Target closes/yr | Cold path hrs | Warm path hrs | Hrs/wk |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | 173 | 173 | 3.6 |
| 4 | 231 | 231 | 4.8 |
| 5 | 288 | 288 | 6.0 |
| 6 | 346 | 346 | 7.2 |

These hours are NET of delivery — they're the dedicated sales motion required to land that volume.

### Channel mix
- **LinkedIn (35% of pipeline target):** 3-5 posts/week per `linkedin-positioning.md`. Content pillars: industry benchmarks (35%), field stories (30%), tactical how-to (25%), POV/contrarian (10%). Cap LinkedIn time at 5 hrs/wk — proof layer, not pipeline.
- **Direct outreach (40%):** Cold + warm. Apollo plugin for prospecting (`apollo:prospect`), Mercury account tracks LinkedIn Premium and Apollo costs.
- **Referrals (15%):** Track NPS at month 3 of every retainer; ask for intros from satisfied clients only.
- **Inbound from content (10%):** Lagging. Won't be material until Y2.

### Content cadence
- LinkedIn: 3-5 posts/week
- Newsletter: bi-weekly to opt-in list (begin Y1)
- Long-form: 1 article/month for SEO + authority

### Sales motion per qualified prospect
1. **Discovery call** (30-45 min, free) — mutual qualification against ICP
2. **Pre-diagnostic intake form** (60-90 min of client time)
3. **Paid diagnostic** ($997 OPS Assessment, 90-min session + doc review + memo)
4. **Proposal** (4-6 pages, PDF, live walkthrough, 7-day expiration)
5. **Agreement + kickoff** within 5 business days of signed + first payment

**Hard rules** (per `engagement-process.md`):
- No proposal without completed diagnostic
- No kickoff without signed agreement AND first month's payment
- No open-ended proposals
- No tier menu in proposals — recommend one path

## 7. Operations Plan

### Delivery model
Retainers are weekly cadence: 1 standing meeting (60 min) + execution work + monthly business review (2 hrs). All client work tracked in ClickUp per `clickup-client-scaffold.md`. Templates and SOPs maintained in `knowledge-base/` Obsidian vault.

### Capacity model

Detailed in `income_forecaster.xlsx` Pipeline & Capacity tab.

| Mode | FW Ops hrs/mo | Sales hrs/mo | Delivery hrs/mo | Max Active retainers |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Solo + Skyright COO (Y1) | 50 | 10 | ~25 | 3 (per CLAUDE.md) |
| Solo full-time (post-Skyright) | 180 | 17 | 111 | 5-6 |
| Solo FT + Mid VA ($24K/yr) | 180 | 8 | 154 | 7-8 |
| Solo FT + Exec VA ($48K/yr) | 180 | 6 | 162 | 8 |
| Solo FT + US Ops Associate ($79K/yr) | 180 | 4 | 166 | 9 |

### Hiring philosophy
**Hire after pipeline, not before.** Most fractional COOs over-staff before they over-sell. Build the SOP library and template stack first; document everything in `knowledge-base/playbooks/` and `_templates/`. Then hire to leverage proven IP, not to build it.

### Recommended sequencing

**Phase 1 (now → Q1-27, while at Skyright):**
Solo. Bottleneck is pipeline, not delivery. No hires. Build SOP library, fill template stack, document IP.

**Phase 2 (Q2-27 → Q4-27, Skyright ending):**
Hire Mid-tier VA at $2,000-2,500/mo ($24-30K/yr). LatAm preferred for time zone (Latamify, Athyna). Deploy on: outreach, admin, follow-up sequences, CRM hygiene, template population. Highest leverage period because Skyright income is gone and every hour matters. Cost = 14-18% of revenue, defensible.

**Phase 3 (Y3 if revenue > $200K with 4+ retainers):**
Upgrade to Executive VA ($3,500-4,500/mo) OR add second Mid VA. Splits admin / deliverables / sales ops.

**Phase 4 (Y4+ only if revenue trends > $300K):**
Junior US Ops Associate ($66-72K base, $79-86K loaded). By then VA stack is mature. Associate is strategy/sales support layer, not admin.

**Phase 5 (Y5+ if revenue > $400K):**
Mid US Associate ($78-85K, $94-102K loaded) + 2 VAs. At this point you're running an agency, not a fractional COO practice — different business model.

## 10. Risk Register

### Critical risks (active monitoring)

**R1 — Client 3 doesn't close.**
- *Likelihood:* Moderate. Client 3 is an assumption, not signed.
- *Impact:* -$27K Y1 revenue, drops Y1 base to $167K
- *Mitigation:* Discovery in early July. If no named prospect by mid-July, formally remove from forecast and replan.

**R2 — Home sale closes below $615K.**
- *Likelihood:* Moderate-high in current FL market
- *Impact:* At $590K, net proceeds drop to $72K. Debt payoff plan must scale back (drop Loan #1).
- *Mitigation:* Pre-priced at conservative target. Run sensitivity at $580K and $560K.

**R3 — No business insurance in place.**
- *Likelihood:* Persistent until quoted
- *Impact:* Single client dispute can wipe out emergency fund in legal fees
- *Mitigation:* Quote Hiscox or Pie Insurance this week. $50-100/mo expected.

**R4 — Skyright W2 ends early (before May-27).**
- *Likelihood:* Low-moderate
- *Impact:* Compresses 12-month bridge to 8-9 months. May-be 2 missing retainers worth of income gap.
- *Mitigation:* Accelerate VA hire and outreach machine. Cut discretionary spend aggressively.

**R5 — Carlos and/or Client 3 churn in Q4.**
- *Likelihood:* 25-30% per industry churn rates
- *Impact:* -$6K/mo if both churn
- *Mitigation:* Emergency fund covers ~6 months of that hole at current burn. Maintain pipeline at all times.

### Strategic risks

**R6 — Pipeline machine doesn't start by Jul-26.**
- *Impact:* Y2 retainer additions miss by 1-2 deals
- *Mitigation:* Outreach + content cadence locked in by mo 2. Track contacts/wk as leading indicator.

**R7 — Sub-vertical decision paralysis.**
- *Impact:* Generic positioning resonates with no one
- *Mitigation:* Roofing first. Document second-vertical decision criteria. No expansion before 4 roofing retainers.

**R8 — Founder LinkedIn time crowds out direct outreach.**
- *Impact:* Vanity metrics, empty pipeline
- *Mitigation:* Cap LinkedIn at 5 hrs/wk. Track conversations, not impressions. Pipeline comes from outreach + referral, not posts.

**R9 — Y2 take-home gap.**
- *Impact:* Family burn could exceed take-home in months with delayed client payments
- *Mitigation:* Emergency fund discipline. AR aging tracked weekly. Net-30 max terms.

**R10 — Quality erosion from over-extension.**
- *Impact:* Reputation damage in tight-knit roofing community
- *Mitigation:* Capacity cap respected. Say "no" or "later" instead of stretching.

### Tail risks (low probability, high impact)

**R11 — Skyright legal or regulatory issue contaminates founder credibility.**
- *Mitigation:* Operate Framework OPS with arm's-length professionalism. Don't use Skyright data, names, processes in marketing.

**R12 — Personal/family health event.**
- *Mitigation:* Disability insurance. Emergency fund. Spouse alignment on cash flow plan.

**R13 — Industry recession in home services.**
- *Mitigation:* Diversify across sub-verticals once first 3-4 retainers are roofing. Mix HVAC and plumbing into Y2 acquisitions.
