# Framework OPS LLC — Fractional COO Offering

## What is a Fractional COO?

A fractional COO is an experienced operations executive who works with your company on a part-time or limited-scope basis. Unlike a full-time hire ($150K–$250K+ salary + benefits), a fractional COO delivers senior-level operational leadership at a fraction of the cost — typically 10–30 hours per month depending on engagement tier.

Framework OPS LLC provides fractional COO services exclusively to home service companies. This is not generalist consulting. The focus is roofing, HVAC, plumbing, landscaping, pest control, and related trades.

### Strategic Execution

- Owner/CEO advisory: decision support, scenario planning, bottleneck removal

## Engagement Models

### Retainer (Most Common)
Ongoing monthly engagement. Fixed hours per month. Ideal for companies that need consistent operational leadership over 6–18 months. Best fit for operators who are growing past $3M and hitting execution ceilings.

### Project-Based
Defined scope with a beginning and end date. Examples: technology implementation, SOP buildout, org redesign, cash flow system install. Typical project duration: 4–12 weeks.

### Strategic Advisory
Low-touch, high-value. Monthly strategy calls, async support, document review. No execution or implementation. For owners who have strong internal operators but need a senior sounding board.
