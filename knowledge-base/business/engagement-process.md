# Framework OPS Engagement Process

## Overview

Every engagement follows the same intake sequence regardless of tier. This is non-negotiable — it protects both parties from misaligned expectations and ensures the diagnosis precedes the prescription.

Total time from first contact to kickoff: typically 2–4 weeks.

---

## Stage 1 — Discovery Call (Week 1)

**Format:** 30–45 minute video call
**Who:** Owner/CEO + Chance Peare
**Goal:** Mutual qualification

### What Gets Covered
- Current revenue, number of employees, service lines
- What's breaking: owner's description of their biggest operational pain points
- What they've tried before (prior consultants, software, hires)
- Why now: growth event, burnout, specific trigger
- Owner's availability and willingness to implement
- Budget range (direct, not coy)

### Framework OPS Evaluates
- Is this the right revenue range ($2M–$20M)?
- Is the owner the actual decision maker?
- Is there implementation capacity (someone to execute changes)?
- Is this a fit for the home service expertise base?
- Red flags: owner blames only external factors, prior consultant carousel, team turnover from culture issues

### Output
- Go / No-Go decision from both sides
- If Go: client completes a pre-diagnostic intake form (sent within 24 hours)

---

## Stage 2 — Pre-Diagnostic Intake (Week 1–2)

**Format:** Structured written questionnaire (sent via form or PDF)
**Time required from client:** 60–90 minutes

### Sections
1. Business overview: revenue, service mix, geography, seasonality
2. Org chart (even if informal): who does what, reporting lines
3. Technology in use: what software, how it's actually used vs. intended
4. Financial snapshot: gross margin %, monthly revenue range, current cash position
5. Top 3 operational problems in owner's own words
6. Key people: any managers or leads, their tenure, performance
7. Goals: what does "success" look like in 12 months?

### Why This Matters
Most consultants skip this and go straight to proposal. That results in proposals that miss the real problem. The intake form forces the owner to articulate problems before the diagnostic, which makes the paid diagnostic more efficient and the proposal sharper.

---

## Stage 3 — Operational Diagnostic (Week 2–3)

**Format:** 90-minute deep-dive video session + document review
**Pricing:** Included in retainer engagements. $2,500–$4,000 as standalone for prospects not ready to commit.

### What Gets Reviewed
- P&L (last 12 months minimum)
- Job costing data if available
- Tech stack walkthrough (screen share of FSM/CRM)
- Sample SOPs or lack thereof
- Cash flow statement or bank statement summary
- Org chart and role clarity
- Close rate and lead source data if tracked

### Output: Diagnostic Summary (delivered within 5 business days)
- 1–2 page written summary of findings
- Priority matrix: top 5 problems ranked by impact vs. effort
- Recommended engagement tier and rationale
- What Framework OPS will NOT address (scope boundaries)

---

## Stage 4 — Proposal (Week 3)

**Format:** PDF proposal delivered via email, reviewed on a 30-minute call
**Length:** 4–6 pages

### Proposal Structure
1. Executive Summary: what we heard, what we see
2. Problem Diagnosis: top 3–5 operational gaps with supporting data
3. Proposed Scope: specific deliverables, not vague promises
4. Engagement Tier and Hours: what Chance does, what client does
5. Timeline: 30/60/90 day milestones
6. Investment: monthly retainer, payment terms, contract length
7. ROI Framing: conservative estimate of value creation

### Proposal Principles
- Do not propose before completing the diagnostic
- Every deliverable should be named specifically (not "improve operations")
- Include a clear next step: "To proceed, sign the agreement and submit first month's retainer by [date]"
- Do not leave proposals open-ended — set a 7-day decision window

---

## Stage 5 — Agreement and Kickoff (Week 3–4)

### Agreement Basics
- Month-to-month after initial commitment period (3 or 6 months)
- Payment: first month due on signing, monthly on same date thereafter
- 30-day written cancellation notice after minimum term
- Confidentiality clause standard
- IP: all templates and tools remain Framework OPS property; client retains docs created for them

### Kickoff Call (60–90 minutes)
Scheduled within 5 business days of signed agreement and received payment.

**Kickoff Agenda:**
1. Confirm goals and success metrics (what does 90 days of success look like?)
2. Communication norms: preferred channels, response time expectations
3. Access gathering: software logins, P&L access, calendar access
4. Stakeholder introductions: who else on the client team needs to know about this engagement?
5. First 30-day priorities: 3 specific focus areas
6. Next scheduled working session date/time confirmed

---

## Stage 6 — Ongoing Engagement

### 30-Day Diagnostic Phase
First 30 days are observation and analysis, not wholesale change. Framework OPS is building the full picture before recommending action. Clients who push for major changes in week 1 should be managed back — premature action before diagnosis creates more chaos.

### 60-Day Quick Win Phase
Days 31–60 focus on 2–3 high-impact, implementable changes. Quick wins build trust with the client team and demonstrate ROI before the 90-day review.

### 90-Day Review
Formal review of progress against initial priorities. Updated roadmap for next 90 days. Retainer renewal decision point if on a 3-month initial term.

### Monthly Reporting
Monthly written summary from Framework OPS: what was worked on, what moved, what's next. This protects against drift and keeps the engagement accountable.
