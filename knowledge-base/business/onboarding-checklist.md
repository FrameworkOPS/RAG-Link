# New Client Onboarding Checklist

## Purpose

This checklist covers everything that needs to happen in the first 30 days of a new client engagement. The goal of onboarding is to: establish trust, get access to what you need to do the work, introduce yourself to the relevant people on the client's team, and deliver the first tangible output within 30 days.

A slow start kills engagements. If the client doesn't see visible progress in the first 30 days, confidence erodes and renewals are at risk.

---

## Pre-Kickoff (Before the Kickoff Call)

### Administrative
- [ ] Signed engagement agreement received
- [ ] First month retainer payment received (do not schedule kickoff until both are in)
- [ ] Client added to invoicing system (QBO) for recurring billing
- [ ] Engagement start date and initial commitment end date logged
- [ ] Kickoff call scheduled (within 5 business days of signed agreement + payment)

### Internal Preparation
- [ ] Pre-intake form reviewed in full
- [ ] Diagnostic summary or discovery call notes reviewed
- [ ] Proposal reviewed — confirm scope and deliverables are clear
- [ ] 30-day kickoff plan drafted (3 specific priorities for Month 1)
- [ ] Client folder created (Drive, Notion, or preferred tool): intake form, proposal, signed agreement, notes

---

## Kickoff Call Agenda (60–90 minutes)

### Welcome and Context (10 min)
- [ ] Confirm the owner's top priorities — do they match the proposal? Any changes since signing?
- [ ] Set expectations: first 30 days are diagnostic and infrastructure, not wholesale change
- [ ] Confirm communication preferences (Slack, email, text, phone) and response time expectations

### Access Gathering (20 min — start during call, complete within 48 hours)
- [ ] QBO or accounting software access (view-only minimum; admin preferred)
- [ ] FSM/CRM access (ServiceTitan, Jobber, HouseCall Pro, etc.)
- [ ] Bank/Mercury read access if available (for cash flow model)
- [ ] Payroll system access (view-only for payroll schedule and costs)
- [ ] Google Drive, Dropbox, or wherever internal documents live
- [ ] Calendar access (to understand owner's schedule and set recurring sessions)
- [ ] Communication tool invite (Slack channel for the engagement)

### Stakeholder Introductions (10 min)
- [ ] Who else on the team needs to know this engagement exists?
- [ ] Are there any managers, office staff, or leads who will be involved in implementation?
- [ ] Who should NOT know yet (sensitive if it's perceived as "the boss hired a fixer")?
- [ ] Plan for how to introduce Framework OPS to the broader team if applicable

### Month 1 Priorities (15 min)
- [ ] Confirm 3 specific priorities for the first 30 days
- [ ] Assign any client-side actions needed to move priority #1 forward
- [ ] Confirm data or documents needed from the client and deadlines

### Logistics (10 min)
- [ ] Confirm recurring weekly/biweekly working session cadence (day, time, duration)
- [ ] Confirm how async questions will be handled
- [ ] Any upcoming vacations, major events, or seasonal peaks to know about?

---

## Week 1–2: Access and Baseline

### Financial Baseline
- [ ] QBO P&L pulled: last 12–24 months
- [ ] Chart of accounts reviewed — identify issues (miscoded expenses, revenue not broken out by service line, etc.)
- [ ] Gross margin calculated by service line if possible
- [ ] AR aging report pulled: note any >60 day balances
- [ ] Cash balance and banking history reviewed
- [ ] Payroll schedule and total monthly payroll cost confirmed

### Operational Baseline
- [ ] FSM walkthrough completed: active jobs, dispatch board, how estimates are handled
- [ ] Job costing data pulled (if available): actual vs. estimated per job
- [ ] Scheduling process documented as-is (before any changes)
- [ ] Tech/crew count, utilization rate (if measurable from FSM)
- [ ] Any existing SOPs or process documentation located and reviewed

### People Baseline
- [ ] Org chart or role list documented (even if informal)
- [ ] Identify key person dependencies: who is irreplaceable right now?
- [ ] Any open positions or recent departures?
- [ ] Turnover in last 12 months noted

---

## Week 2–3: First Deliverables

### KPI Dashboard (Priority in Month 1 for almost every client)
- [ ] Identify 8–12 KPIs from the KPI library relevant to this client
- [ ] Determine data sources for each metric (FSM, QBO, manual)
- [ ] Build baseline version in Google Sheets or equivalent
- [ ] Train client-side person on how to update it weekly
- [ ] First review of dashboard on next working session

### Cash Flow Model (If cash flow is a stated concern)
- [ ] 13-week rolling cash flow model built
- [ ] Opening balance, recurring outflows, and open AR entered
- [ ] Train office manager or owner on weekly update process
- [ ] Walk through model on next working session

### Quick Win Identification
- [ ] Identify 1–2 quick wins achievable in first 30 days
  - Examples: activate review request automation, set up appointment reminders, fix a QBO coding issue, write one SOP
- [ ] Quick wins selected should: be fast to implement, require minimal resources, produce visible results
- [ ] At least one quick win delivered before Day 30

---

## End of Month 1: 30-Day Check-In

- [ ] Written 30-day summary sent to client: what was assessed, what was found, what was built
- [ ] Priority matrix updated: top 5 problems, recommended sequencing for Month 2–3
- [ ] 90-day roadmap draft shared for owner review and alignment
- [ ] Quick win results shared (review volume, cash flow model output, etc.)
- [ ] Client satisfaction check: "How are things going? Is this what you expected? Anything to adjust?"
- [ ] Next 30-day priorities confirmed

---

## Ongoing Monthly Rhythm (Post-Onboarding)

- [ ] Weekly working session (standing appointment)
- [ ] Monthly written summary: what was worked on, what moved, what's next
- [ ] Monthly KPI review (15–30 min session)
- [ ] AR aging review if DSO is a concern
- [ ] Quarterly business review (formal, 90 minutes): progress against roadmap, goal reset, renewal discussion if applicable

---

## Onboarding Risk Flags

**Flag these early if present — address directly, not indirectly:**

- Client hasn't sent requested access after 72 hours → send reminder, if 7 days no response, get on a call and ask why
- Owner isn't showing up to working sessions → engagement is at risk; have the conversation in month 1, not month 3
- Team resistance to changes → understand the dynamics; may need to adjust communication approach or involve owner in change communication
- "I don't have time to work on this stuff" from the client → this will kill the engagement; clarify time commitment expectations immediately
- Client adding scope without adjusting compensation → document everything and address in the monthly summary
