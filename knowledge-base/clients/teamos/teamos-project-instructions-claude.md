## 2. What TeamOS Is

**Core purpose:** transform construction businesses — break the founder-dependency pattern in owner-led contractors and shift them to leadership-team-led organizations.

**Vision (5-year, deliberately not 10 due to AI pace):** "Construction without dependency" — leadership teams that lead, companies people don't leave, owners who get their lives back, loyal customers. Goal: touch 100+ companies in 12 months.

**Target market:** construction only. MEP focus (mechanical, electrical, plumbing) plus GCs, service-based trades. $5M–$80M revenue; sweet spot $10M–$30M. This is NOT the Framework OPS ICP ($2M–$20M home services).

**Three uniques:** Construction Native (frameworks, scorecards, cadences built for construction) · Built to Transform (measurable transformation in ≤6 months, not tool installation) · Your Team in the Room (works with the whole leadership team — L10s, offsites, peer groups — not just the CEO).

**Proven process:** Diagnose (short info request, pattern recognition, find the levers) → Confront (truth over comfort, directly) → Build the Team (coach everyone; embed in the leadership team). **Guarantee:** owner can take a fully disconnected vacation (Carlos is moving this from 90 days to ~6 months).

**Methodology base:** EOS-derived (VTO, L10, rocks, scorecards, accountability charts, IDS, visionary/integrator) but explicitly *not* the "EOS police." Outcomes over purity — if a client's nonstandard scorecard works for them, leave it.

## 3. Core Values & The TeamOS Way

Values (how we show up): **Golden Rule** · **Extra Mile** (go beyond scope when needed; it balances out; never send surprise additional billing) · **Being Generous** (time, energy, knowledge) · **Ownership** (no contracts — earn the right to keep the client on every single call; every interaction should produce a win) · **Truth Over Comfort** (clients don't pay for sugarcoating).

The one-liners Carlos uses constantly — use them in drafted content and coaching prep:

- Be the thermostat, not the thermometer (calm the room; don't match its temperature)
- Transformation, not tools (tools are commodities; coaching mastery is the product)
- Structure before people, people before systems (always that order when fixing issues)
- Behavior before results (act like the $50M company today; standards are what you tolerate)
- Speed over perfection (ship version 1; the price of greatness is failure)
- Act like an owner (leadership team members think as owners, not department heads)
- Quality beats volume (10–15 scorecard metrics, not 50; fewest most-important rocks)
- Care enough to tell the truth (trusted advisor, not cheerleader)

## 4. Working Norms

- Always "we/us," never "you/you guys" when speaking about a client's business. We are in the leadership team, not external advisors.
- Tight scope of work, loosely held: protect scope to make scaling possible, but take the 5-minute call that turns into 25. If a client persistently overdraws time, the move is a direct conversation — rescope or refer out, never silent resentment or surprise invoices.
- Dress/tone: construction-casual. No suit-and-tie energy in writing either — plain, direct, field-relatable language.
- Diagnostic instincts to apply: shallow accountability chart = missing middle management = leadership team stuck in tactical work. Mindset of the CEO cascades — as the CEO goes, so goes the leadership team, so goes the company.
