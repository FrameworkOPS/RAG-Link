# Roofing Operations Benchmarks

## Revenue and Growth

| Metric | Benchmark Range | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue per salesperson (residential) | $800K–$1.5M/year | Top performers exceed $2M |
| Revenue per production crew | $600K–$1.2M/year | Varies by market and job mix |
| Revenue per tech/installer | $250K–$450K/year | Lower end = commercial-heavy |
| Annual revenue growth (healthy growth co.) | 15–30% | Above 30% = scaling risk without systems |

---

## Sales and Close Rates

| Metric | Benchmark | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Residential close rate (in-home estimate) | 35–55% | Below 35% = sales process or pricing issue |
| Storm-centric close rate | 55–75% | Insurance work has higher close rates |
| Average revenue per job (residential re-roof) | $8,000–$18,000 | Market-dependent; higher in coastal/storm areas |
| Average revenue per job (commercial) | $25,000–$200,000+ | Wide variance |
| Lead-to-estimate conversion | 60–80% | If below 60%, canvassing or marketing quality issue |
| Estimate-to-close cycle time | 1–14 days | Storm market: often same-day; retail: 3–7 days |

---

## Job Costing and Gross Margin

| Metric | Benchmark | Red Flag |
|---|---|---|
| Gross margin (residential re-roof) | 38–52% | Below 35% = likely underpriced or over-labored |
| Gross margin (commercial) | 28–40% | Higher material cost, more bidding risk |
| Materials as % of job revenue | 30–40% | Varies heavily by shingle brand and market |
| Labor as % of job revenue | 18–28% | Subcontracted vs. in-house affects this significantly |
| Subcontractor labor cost vs. employee crew | Sub often 5–10% higher margin risk | Depends on crew utilization rate |
| Overhead as % of revenue | 18–28% | Above 30% = company cannot scale profitably |
| Net profit margin (well-run roofing company) | 8–15% | Below 5% = unsustainable |

---

## Production and Crew Efficiency

| Metric | Benchmark | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Squares per crew per day (residential) | 15–25 squares | Depends on pitch, complexity, crew size |
| Crew utilization rate (days working vs. days available) | 75–85% | Below 70% = scheduling or sales pipeline issue |
| Job cycle time (signed contract to completion) | 2–21 days | Storm market can be 60–90 days due to adjuster delays |
| Callbacks/warranty calls as % of jobs | <2% | Above 3% = installation quality problem |
| Material waste/overage per job | 8–12% | Above 15% = estimating problem |

---

## Accounts Receivable and Cash Flow

| Metric | Benchmark | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Days Sales Outstanding (DSO) | 15–35 days | Insurance jobs often 45–60 days |
| Deposit collected at signing | 30–50% | Non-insurance; never start without a deposit |
| Final payment collected at completion | Day of or within 7 days | |
| Aged receivables >60 days | <5% of total AR | Above 10% = collections issue |
| Material float (days between PO and job revenue) | 7–21 days | Key cash flow risk in high-volume growth periods |

---

## Marketing and Lead Generation

| Metric | Benchmark | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Cost per lead (digital) | $50–$200 | Varies widely by market and competition |
| Cost per lead (canvassing/door knock) | $20–$80 | Labor-intensive but effective in storm markets |
| Cost per acquired customer (total marketing spend / jobs closed) | $300–$1,000 | Storm market can be lower |
| Marketing as % of revenue | 5–12% | Below 5% = typically referral-dependent (risky) |
| Customer acquisition cost payback period | Same job | Unlike SaaS, roofing LTV is low (1–2 jobs per customer lifetime) |

---

## Key Ratios for a Healthy Roofing Company

A roofing company at $5M in annual revenue should look approximately like:
- Gross margin: ~43% → $2.15M gross profit
- Overhead: ~22% → $1.1M
- Owner compensation (market rate): ~$150K–$200K included in overhead
- Net profit before tax: ~8–12% → $400K–$600K

If gross margin is below 38% or overhead is above 30%, the company is either underpriced, overheaded, or both. These are the first two things a fractional COO should measure.
