# HVAC Operations Benchmarks

## Revenue and Productivity

| Metric | Benchmark Range | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue per tech (service/maintenance) | $150K–$300K/year | Top performers $350K+ |
| Revenue per tech (installation) | $300K–$600K/year | Install crews run higher per-person revenue |
| Revenue per service call | $250–$600 | Diagnostic + repair; maintenance agreements lower per-call |
| Calls per tech per day (service) | 4–7 | Urban markets trend lower due to drive time |
| Revenue per truck (all-in) | $200K–$450K/year | Key fleet utilization metric |

---

## Gross Margin by Revenue Type

| Revenue Stream | Gross Margin Benchmark | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Service calls (T&M repair) | 55–70% | Highest margin work in HVAC |
| Maintenance agreements (labor only) | 45–60% | Recurring, predictable |
| Equipment installation (residential) | 30–45% | Equipment cost is significant |
| Commercial service contracts | 35–50% | Volume-based, longer-term |
| Parts and materials (retail markup) | 40–60% | If selling parts at retail vs. cost |
| Overall blended gross margin | 40–55% | Well-run HVAC should land here |

---

## Service Agreement (Maintenance Contract) Benchmarks

| Metric | Benchmark | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Service agreement penetration rate | 20–40% of active customers | Best-in-class companies hit 40%+ |
| Annual revenue per agreement | $150–$350/customer | Depends on inclusions (filters, tune-ups) |
| Agreement renewal rate | 70–85% | Below 70% = pricing or delivery issue |
| Customer lifetime value (with agreement) | 3–5x higher than transactional customer | Core driver for HVAC valuation |
| Revenue per service agreement customer (total, not just agreement) | $500–$1,200/year | Agreements create access for upsells |

---

## Sales Performance

| Metric | Benchmark | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Equipment replacement close rate (in-home) | 35–55% | Presented by service tech or comfort advisor |
| Average ticket: residential system replacement | $8,000–$18,000 | SEER rating, brand, labor market |
| Tune-up to service agreement conversion | 25–45% | If selling agreements from maintenance visits |
| Accessory attach rate (IAQ, smart thermostats) | 15–30% of jobs | Often undertrained and underpursued |
| Service-to-install upsell rate (tech to comfort advisor) | 20–35% | Depends on company's sales culture |

---

## Scheduling and Dispatch Efficiency

| Metric | Benchmark | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Same-day/next-day appointment fill rate | 85–95% (peak season) | Emergency calls should be same-day |
| Dispatch efficiency (route optimization) | <20% of tech time in windshield | Poor scheduling = lost capacity |
| First-call resolution rate | 80–90% | Below 80% = parts availability or diagnostic training issue |
| Average call-to-arrival time (emergency) | <4 hours | Competitive differentiator in most markets |
| Return trip rate (warranty/redo) | <5% | Above 7% = quality control issue |

---

## Financial Ratios

| Metric | Benchmark | Red Flag |
|---|---|---|
| Labor as % of revenue | 30–40% | Above 42% = overstaffed or underbilled |
| Materials/parts as % of revenue | 15–25% | Highly variable by service mix |
| Overhead as % of revenue | 20–28% | Above 30% = scaling problem |
| Net profit margin | 8–15% | Industry average closer to 10% |
| Owner compensation as % of revenue | 3–7% at $3M–$10M | Should normalize with company scale |

---

## Seasonal Revenue Distribution (Typical)

| Quarter | Revenue Share | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 (Jan–Mar) | 15–20% | Heating shoulder season, slower |
| Q2 (Apr–Jun) | 25–30% | Pre-season tune-ups, installs |
| Q3 (Jul–Sep) | 30–40% | Peak cooling demand; max capacity |
| Q4 (Oct–Dec) | 15–20% | Heating season, maintenance |

HVAC companies with heavy residential AC markets in Sun Belt states will see Q3 disproportionately large (35–45% of revenue). This creates acute cash flow and staffing challenges. Companies without a maintenance agreement revenue base are highly exposed to weather variability.

---

## Key Operational Benchmarks for a $5M HVAC Company

At $5M revenue, a well-run residential HVAC company should have:
- 8–12 service technicians
- 2–4 installation crews
- Blended gross margin: ~47%
- Overhead: ~25% ($1.25M)
- Net profit: ~10–15% ($500K–$750K)
- 400–600 active maintenance agreements

If gross margin is below 42%, the company is either pricing too low, running inefficient service calls, or not selling agreements (which blends the margin up).
