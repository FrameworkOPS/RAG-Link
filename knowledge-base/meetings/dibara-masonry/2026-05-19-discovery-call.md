# Meeting: DiBara Masonry — 2026-05-19

## Decisions

- Move from Discovery (Stage 1) directly into proposal track, with **OPS Assessment positioned as Phase 0**. Preserves the "no proposal without diagnostic" rule by making the diagnostic the first paid deliverable inside the master proposal.
- **Bid scope:** Active Retainer (base) + optional HubSpot Service Hub Build add-on. Do not pursue Sales Hub build — that's the marketing agency's lane; competing there poisons the relationship and the brand work.
- Retainer tier hypothesis: **Active ($4K/mo)**. Embedded ($6K/mo) may be the more honest tier given the depth of gaps, but Chance's capacity constraints + this being an early engagement argue for Active with an explicit upgrade path documented in the diagnostic report.
- **First-deal pricing and structure decisions (post-discovery, Chance's call):**
  - **Phase numbering:** Phase 0 = OPS Assessment; **Phase 1 = Active Retainer**; **Optional Phase = HubSpot Service Hub Build** (no number — designated optional).
  - **Timing:** Phase 1 retainer kicks off **Month 1** (immediately after Phase 0 diagnostic in Weeks 1–2). If elected, Optional Phase HubSpot build runs concurrent with Phase 1 in Month 1.
  - **Optional Phase pricing:** $3,500 bundled rate when paired with Phase 1 signing (vs. ~$12,500 standalone). Tightened scope ~25 hours: pipeline + 2–3 core automations + basic dashboard + 1 training session. If declined, the production-CRM gap is absorbed into Phase 1 retainer hours over Months 1–3.
  - **Retainer is month-to-month, no 6-month minimum lock-in.** Month 1 committed at signing, 30-day notice to end thereafter.
  - **Base committed at signing: $4,000.** With Optional Phase: $7,500. Expected probability-weighted engagement value: ~$20,700. Best case (full 12-month retainer): $51,500.
  - Tradeoffs accepted: Optional Phase revenue at risk (DiBara may decline); ~$13K standalone-scope value foregone on the build if they take it; ~$16K-$23K of retainer revenue at risk without a lock-in. Justified by (a) first-deal positioning, (b) Phase 0 diagnostic gating real exposure.
- **Owner sales-dependence reframe (Chance's call):** Do NOT position the failed sales hiring as a "broken systems" problem in the proposal. Position it as structural owner-dependence with the **rigid, formal, documented sales training program** as the lever to release the owner. Reasoning: blaming infrastructure for hiring failures puts the owner on the defensive about decisions he's already made.

## Follow-Up Questions

These need answers before or during the Operational Diagnostic. They directly affect retainer scoping and the ROI math in the proposal:

1. **What is gross margin actually doing trailing 12 mo and 24 mo?** Masonry typically runs 25–35% GM. If DiBara is materially below, that's the headline number for the ROI section.
2. **Residential vs. commercial mix?** Commercial brings payment-term lag (60–90 days) — cash flow modeling assumptions change significantly.
3. **Average job size? Job count per year?** Drives whether job costing should be done at job level vs. project / phase level.
4. **Who actually creates and owns the work order today?** If the answer is "the sub" — that is the single biggest margin leak in the company. Confirm.
5. **What's the current sales close rate and average sales cycle?** Determines whether the Sales Hub build (if it ever falls in scope) is worth pursuing.
6. **Has the owner tried to hire a COO before?** If yes — what happened? Failed COO hire is a buying trigger but also a red flag for "consultant carousel" pattern.
7. **What's the budget conversation that's already happened?** Owner mentioned exploring relocation for sales hires — implies meaningful budget. Anchor the proposal accordingly but don't over-price.
8. **Marketing agency relationship:** retainer vs. project? Who owns the HubSpot account once they're done? If the agency keeps admin, that's a future fight.
