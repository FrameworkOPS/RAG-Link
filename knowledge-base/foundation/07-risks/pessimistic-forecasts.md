## Outreach math reality

First 30 days post-launch (July 5 - Aug 5, 2026):

### Cold email
- 500-1,000 cold sends to ICP list
- 1-3% reply rate at this list quality = 5-30 replies
- 20-30% of interested replies → discovery call = 1-9 calls
- **Realistic: 3-5 cold email discovery calls**

### LinkedIn outreach
- 100-300 connection requests
- 30-50% acceptance rate = 30-150 new connections
- 2-5% conversion to conversation = 1-7 conversations
- **Realistic: 2-4 LinkedIn discovery calls**

### Warm network
- 30 personalized asks to top warm relationships
- 30-50% reply rate = 9-15 replies
- 50-70% of replies → discovery call = 5-10 calls
- **Realistic: 5-10 warm network discovery calls**

### Total expected
**10-19 discovery calls in first 30 days.**

### Conversion expectations
- Discovery → Audit ($997): 20-30% close rate = 2-6 audits closed
- Audit → Phase 1 / Retainer: 50-60% = 1-4 deeper engagements
- **First 30 days realistic revenue: $5-15K**

## Cash flow reality

Year-1 pricing means thin margin on hourly basis. Real economics:

| Offer | Price | Realistic hours | Effective hourly |
|---|---|---|---|
| OPS Assessment | $997 | 10-12 | $83-100/hr |
| SOP Sprint | $2,000 | 12-15 | $133-167/hr |
| Automation Build | $2,000 | 15-20 | $100-133/hr |
| Advisory | $1,500/mo | 4-6/mo | $250-375/hr |
| OPS Light | $2,500/mo | 8-12/mo | $210-310/hr |
| OPS Active | $4,000/mo | 15-20/mo | $200-265/hr |
| OPS Embedded | $6,000/mo | 30-40/mo | $150-200/hr |

**Productized offers (Audit/Sprint/Build) are nearly break-even hourly vs. roofing COO salary.** They exist for client acquisition + case study creation, not profit.

**Retainers are the actual profit engine** at $150-375/hr effective depending on tier (Embedded is the lowest hourly — it earns on depth and commitment length, not rate).

This means: every hour spent on Audit/Sprint/Build delivery is a marketing/acquisition cost, not a profit center. Budget accordingly.

## Time-to-replace-income reality

Most likely scenario:

- Month 6: $2-4K/mo recurring
- Month 12: $7-10K/mo recurring
- Month 18: $10-13K/mo recurring
- Month 24: $13-16K/mo recurring

**Realistic full income replacement: month 24-30.**

If 6-month exit from roofing role is fixed: need 12+ months runway saved OR partner channel acceleration OR Year 1 price increase pulled forward.

## What this means for decisions

1. **Don't quit the roofing job early.** Realistic income replacement is 18-30 months.
2. **Don't discount Year 1 pricing.** Already low. Lower means below break-even hourly.
3. **Don't refuse productized work because hourly rate is low.** It's acquisition cost.
4. **Don't expand offer ladder.** Six offers + focus > eleven offers + diluted attention.
5. **Don't skip case study capture.** Without it, Year 1 underpricing has no upside.
