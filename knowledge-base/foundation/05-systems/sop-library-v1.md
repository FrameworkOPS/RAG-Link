# SOP Library v1

15-20 core home service SOPs built generically + customizable. Week 6 deliverable.

## Why this matters

- Every $1-3M home service operator has these gaps
- Building generically means 70%+ reusable per engagement
- Major time saver: 15-20 hr per client during Phase 1 implementations

## SOP list

### Sales & Marketing
1. **Lead intake** — channels, response SLAs, qualification questions, routing
2. **Sales handoff** — info captured at sale → required for ops handoff
3. **Quote/estimate workflow** — process from inspection to delivered quote
4. **Customer follow-up** — post-quote, post-job touchpoints
5. **Review request** — when, how, escalation for unhappy customers

### Operations
6. **Job scheduling** — capacity planning, customer comms, change handling
7. **Dispatch protocol** — daily routing, real-time changes, communication
8. **Job completion checklist** — what techs must do/document before closing
9. **Quality control inspection** — post-job verification, callback protocol
10. **Truck/equipment inventory** — daily/weekly checks, restocking

### Finance & Admin
11. **Collections** — billing cadence, payment options, escalation
12. **Monthly financial close** — bookkeeping, reporting, owner review
13. **Vendor management** — onboarding, payment terms, performance review

### HR & Hiring
14. **Hiring workflow** — sourcing, screening, interviews, offers
15. **Employee onboarding** — week 1-30 plan, training, check-ins
16. **Weekly leadership meeting** — agenda template, decision tracking

### Customer Experience
17. **Customer onboarding** — post-sale to first job
18. **Complaint resolution** — intake, investigation, resolution, follow-up
19. **Safety incident reporting** — documentation, escalation, follow-up
20. **Permit / compliance tracking** — licenses, certifications, deadlines

## SOP standard format

Every SOP follows same structure:

1. **Purpose** — 1 sentence on why this SOP exists
2. **Owner** — role responsible (not person)
3. **Trigger** — when this SOP fires
4. **Inputs** — what's needed to start
5. **Steps** — numbered, action-oriented
6. **Outputs** — what's produced
7. **Exceptions** — common edge cases + how to handle
8. **Tools** — software/forms/templates used
9. **Metrics** — how to measure if SOP is working
10. **Last reviewed** — date + reviewer

## Customization per client

Generic SOP + client-specific:
- Their tools (e.g., Housecall Pro vs ServiceTitan vs Jobber)
- Their team structure
- Their specific examples
- Their pricing/quote ranges

Estimated customization time per SOP: 30-60 min.

## Sub-vertical variations

Some SOPs vary significantly by sub-vertical:
- Roofing: insurance claims, supplement processes, manufacturer warranties
- HVAC: maintenance contracts, refrigerant tracking, seasonal demand mgmt
- Plumbing: emergency response protocols, water damage liability

**Build approach:** Generic version first. Add sub-vertical variants only as needed (after first client per vertical).

## Reusability tracking

Per [[clickup-hierarchy]] Custom fields, track:
- Reusability Score 1-5 (1 = highly client-specific, 5 = drop-in ready)
- Vertical Applicability (Roofing / HVAC / Plumbing / All)
- Usage count (how many clients have used this)

After 3 clients: identify which SOPs are 5/5 reusable. Those become the "starter pack" for new engagements via [[engagement-template-generator]].
