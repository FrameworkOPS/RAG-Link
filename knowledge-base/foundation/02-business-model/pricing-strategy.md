# Pricing Strategy

## Current approach: Aggressive for reputation (Year 1)

Year 1 strategy is **deliberate underpricing** to accelerate:
- Case study capture
- Testimonial volume
- Referral velocity
- Reps running own systems live

The salaried roofing COO role covers the financial nut, so margin can be traded for proof.

## Defensible because

- No need for the money immediately
- Lower prices = faster sales cycles, less buyer scrutiny
- $1-3M owners think harder about $5K than $3K — friction matters
- Year-1 clients are reputation builders, not steady-state customer base

## Risks of this approach

### Price anchoring
Clients who anchor at today's Year-1 rates (e.g. $4,000/mo Active) will resist a materially higher renewal price. **Plan to graduate clients out, not raise them up.**

### Cheap clients are often the worst clients
$1-2M owners writing $1,500 Advisory checks scrutinize every hour. Budget extra hand-holding time at the lowest tier.

### Stuck-at-low-prices trap
Without a planned increase milestone, year-3 prices will match year-1 prices because raising on existing clients is hard.

## Planned price increase trigger

> **"Once I have 3 completed case studies + leave the roofing COO role, all new clients are at Tier 2 pricing."**

Tier 2 target pricing (rough — to be refined when trigger hits):
- OPS Assessment: $997 → $2,500
- SOP Sprint: $2,000 → $2,500
- Automation Build: $2,000 → $3,500
- OPS Light: $2,500 → $3,000
- OPS Active: $4,000 → $5,000
- OPS Embedded: $6,000 → $8,500

Existing clients grandfather at Year-1 rates for 12 months, then transition.

## Market data context

- Fractional COO retainers across all segments: $3-15K/month
- $1-3M home service specifically: realistic ceiling $3-6K/month
- Year-1 Framework OPS retainers ($1.5-6K) are at low end of market — intentional
- Year-2 target pricing ($3-8.5K) is mid-market — appropriate given credentials + case studies

## What NOT to do

- Don't offer discounts on top of already-low prices
- Don't bundle to lower effective rate ("audit + sprint package for $2,500" destroys margin)
- Don't extend payment terms on retainers (monthly upfront, no exceptions)
- Don't quote ranges on productized offers — flat fees only
