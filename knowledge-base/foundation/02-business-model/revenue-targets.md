# Revenue Targets

## Primary target

**$150K cumulative revenue by end of year 2027.**

Equivalent to replacing approximate $150K income while rolling into 2027.

## Realistic projection (pessimistic baseline)

At aggressive Year-1 pricing, ICP execution, and disciplined outreach starting July 5, 2026:

| Period | Months | Activity | Revenue |
|---|---|---|---|
| May-Oct 2026 | 1-6 | 2-3 audits, 1-2 sprints, 1 OPS Light retainer mid-period | $15-25K |
| Nov 2026-Apr 2027 | 7-12 | 2 OPS Light + 1 OPS Active running, 3-4 sprints, steady audit flow | $50-70K |
| May-Dec 2027 | 13-20 | 2 OPS Active + 1 OPS Light/Embedded, sprint cadence, regular audits | $80-100K |

**Total cumulative through EOY 2027: $145-195K. Midpoint: $165K.**

Hits target if execution is clean.

## Critical caveat — run rate vs cumulative

By EOY 2027, **monthly run rate is only $12-15K**. To sustain $150K/year as steady-state income (not cumulative), one of these must happen:

1. Mid-2027 price increase (per [[pricing-strategy]] trigger)
2. 4+ concurrent retainers (capacity ceiling — risky while still at roofing job)
3. Scale via team/templates (Year 2+ play)

**$150K cumulative by EOY 2027 ≠ $150K/year steady state.**

## Failure scenarios

### Underperformance scenario (likely if pricing not enforced)
- Same activity at discounted/unenforced pricing: $90-120K cumulative
- Misses target by $30-60K

### Outreach delay scenario
- Every 1 month delay in launch costs ~$8-15K in Year 1 revenue
- 2-month delay (launch Sept instead of July) = miss target by $20-30K

### Single-client scenario
- If Daniel is the only client through Q3 2026: cumulative $40-50K total
- Below target by $100K+

## What needs to be true for target to hit

1. Outreach starts July 5, 2026 (no slippage)
2. First 3 case studies captured by Q4 2026
3. Pricing held — no discounting Year 1
4. Sub-vertical chosen and consistently pursued
5. At least 3 active retainers running by month 12
6. Daniel engagement converts to public case study (single biggest sales asset)

## What I'm not modeling

- Cost of customer acquisition (assumed minimal at this stage — warm network + organic LinkedIn)
- Refunds or scope disputes (low at $1-3K price points but not zero)
- Tax implications (consult CPA)
- Reinvestment into tools/team
