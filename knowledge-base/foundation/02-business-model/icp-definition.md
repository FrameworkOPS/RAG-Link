# Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

## Target

**$1-3M annual revenue home service operators.**

## Characteristics

- Owner-operator running lean ops
- 5-25 employees typically
- Owner is the operational bottleneck
- $150-400K annual owner take-home
- No formal ops manager or COO in place
- Has some tech stack but underutilized (CRM, scheduling tool, etc.)
- Growing past their ability to manage operationally

## Sub-vertical focus

**TBD — pending decision before Week 1 checkpoint (May 17, 2026).**

Options:
- **Roofing-only:** Strongest existing network + credibility. Caps ICP pool.
- **HVAC:** More $1-3M operators nationally. Less direct expertise.
- **Plumbing:** Highest desperation-for-systems quotient. Smaller market.
- **Mixed home services:** Broader funnel. Risks generic positioning.

Recommendation: **Start with one sub-vertical. Roofing is the obvious play given existing network.**

## What this ICP can/cannot afford

- Price-sensitive on advisory spend even when desperately needing help
- Realistic ceiling on retainers: $3-6K/mo; significant resistance above $5K
- Realistic ceiling on audits/assessments: $2-4K before pushback
- Aggressive year-1 pricing (see [[pricing-strategy]]) trades margin for reps

## Disqualifying criteria

- Under $1M revenue: cannot afford the engagement
- Over $4M revenue: usually wants full-time hire or too complex for side practice while still at roofing job
- No software/tech in use at all: too much lift to build from zero
- Owner who refuses to commit time to working sessions: predictable failure
