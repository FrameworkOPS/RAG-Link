# Contract Requirements

Mandatory clauses on every Framework OPS engagement, regardless of tier.

## 1. Case study + testimonial clause

> "Upon successful completion of the engagement, Client agrees to participate in a 30-minute case study interview and provide a written testimonial suitable for marketing use."

**Why mandatory:** Aggressive Year-1 pricing only builds reputation if case studies actually get captured. Without contractual commitment, clients ghost the post-mortem.

**Trigger:** "Successful completion" defined as deliverables shipped per SOW. Not contingent on outcomes (which client controls execution of).

## 2. Scope change order policy

> "Work outside the documented Scope of Work requires a written change order, billed at $250/hour or $2,000 per added workflow, whichever is greater."

**Why mandatory:** Daniel engagement already taught the scope creep lesson. Every fixed-fee engagement needs this guardrail.

**Operational rule:** Verbal scope additions are NOT accepted. "I'll get that to you Monday" without a change order is a scope creep moment. Redirect: "Happy to scope that — let me send a change order today."

## 3. Out-of-scope explicitly defined

Every SOW lists what's NOT included. Generic examples:

- Ongoing operational management (after engagement ends)
- Direct team management (hiring/firing)
- Financial controllership
- Legal advice
- Recruiting (vs. hiring framework support)
- Additional automations beyond agreed count
- Additional SOPs beyond agreed count

**Why mandatory:** Owners default to assuming you'll do "whatever needs doing." Explicit out-of-scope language is the only defense.

## 4. Payment terms

- **Productized offers (Assessment, Sprint, Build):** 50% upfront, 50% on delivery. No exceptions.
- **Retainers:** Monthly upfront on the 1st. Late payment = pause work until current.
- **No net-30, no net-15.** This ICP doesn't have that cash flow profile and chasing payment is a tax on the practice.

## 5. Cancellation / termination

- **Sprints:** Non-refundable once kicked off. Pre-kickoff: 50% refund minus admin.
- **Retainers (3-mo min):** Cancellation after minimum requires 30 days written notice.
- **Retainers (6-mo min, Embedded):** Cancellation after minimum requires 60 days written notice.

## 6. IP and confidentiality

- Frameworks, SOPs, and templates Chance brings in remain Framework OPS IP. Client gets license to use.
- Client-specific deliverables (their SOPs, their automations) become Client IP.
- Standard mutual NDA included.

## 7. Communication SLAs

- **Email/Slack:** Same business day acknowledgment, 24-hour substantive response.
- **Async (Loom):** 48-hour turnaround.
- **Working sessions:** Scheduled in advance, no ad-hoc.

## Legal review reminder

This document is a working spec, not a legal contract. **All SOWs and retainer agreements require attorney review before sending.** Especially:
- State-specific consulting agreement requirements
- Indemnification language
- Limitation of liability
- Dispute resolution / venue

Use a small business attorney familiar with consulting practices, not a general practice attorney.
