# Offer Ladder

Final offer structure as of May 2026. **6 offers total.** All previous "Entry & Trust Builder" micro-offers killed in pricing simplification.

## Tier 1: Entry (1 offer)

### OPS Assessment — $997 flat

- **Format:** 2-3 day focused deep dive
- **Promise:** Walk away with prioritized audit of what's broken, what's fixable, 90-day action plan.
- **Positioning:** "Start Here" on site
- **Credit:** Applied toward first retainer month if client continues
- **Delivery time after templates built:** 10-12 hours
- **Why this exists:** Front door + qualification mechanism. Low-friction entry that filters serious prospects.

## Tier 2: Productized Sprints (2 offers)

### SOP Sprint — $1,500 flat

- **Format:** 2 weeks
- **Scope:** One focused department or process, fully documented
- **Deliverable:** SOP doc + training video + rollout plan
- **Hours to deliver:** 12-15 (watch scope creep — at 25 hrs this is $60/hr, worse than roofing job)
- **Examples:** Estimating workflow, job completion checklist, dispatch protocol

### Automation Build — $2,000 flat

- **Format:** 2-3 weeks
- **Scope:** One workflow automated end-to-end
- **Deliverable:** Live automation + handoff docs
- **Hours to deliver:** 15-20
- **Examples:** Lead follow-up, job completion trigger, review request sequence

## Tier 3: Fractional COO Retainers (3 offers)

### OPS Light — $2,000/month

- **Hours:** 8-10/mo, weekly cadence
- **Minimum:** 3-month commitment
- **For:** $1-2M owners systematizing one area at a time
- **Effective rate:** $200-250/hr

### OPS Active — $3,500/month ⭐ MOST COMMON

- **Hours:** 16-20/mo, weekly + project work
- **Minimum:** 3-month commitment
- **For:** $2-3M owners with active build needs across process, tech, accountability
- **Effective rate:** $175-220/hr
- **This is the bread and butter.**

### OPS Embedded — $5,500/month

- **Hours:** 25-30/mo (capped — do not let bloat to 40), multiple touchpoints/week
- **Minimum:** 6-month commitment
- **For:** $2.5-4M owners needing real COO horsepower without full-time hire
- **Effective rate:** $183-220/hr
- **Note:** Reduced from original $6,500/30-40hr because the highest-paying tier was the worst hourly rate. Cap hours, don't let it bloat.

## Killed offers (do not reintroduce)

- AI Readiness Score ($297) — too cheap to be worth the sale friction
- Tech Stack Audit ($497) — overlaps with OPS Assessment
- 90-Day OPS Roadmap ($2,000) — overlaps with OPS Assessment and retainers
- OPS Advisory ($1,200) — attracted wrong clients, didn't pay for time

## Service ladder logic

Audit ($997) → Sprint ($1,500-2,000) → Retainer ($2,000-5,500/mo)

Goal: $997 audit converts at 50-60% to next-step engagement. If under 40%, audit isn't selling the next step properly.

## Mandatory clauses on every engagement

See [[contract-requirements]]:
- Case study + testimonial clause
- Scope change order policy ($250/hr or $2K per added workflow)
- Out-of-scope explicitly defined
