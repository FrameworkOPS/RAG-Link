# Offer Ladder

Current offer structure. **10 offers across 3 groups.** Canonical pricing source is [[service-tiers]] in `business/` — this file mirrors it for the foundation set. If the two ever disagree, `business/service-tiers.md` wins.

> **History:** A May 2026 "pricing simplification" briefly cut the menu to 6 offers and killed the entry products. That was reversed — the three entry products are live again, Advisory was re-added as the lowest retainer tier, and a third sprint (KPI Dashboard Build) was added. Do not re-cut the menu without updating `service-tiers.md` first.

## Group 1: Entry / Trust Builders (3 offers)

Low-risk, defined deliverables. Front door + qualification mechanism that filters serious prospects and surfaces retainer candidates.

### AI Readiness Assessment — $297 flat

- **Format:** Async. Structured intake form → 2-3 page written report within 5 business days + one 30-min review call
- **Deliverable:** Where the client stands on AI/automation adoption + prioritized list of 5-8 automation opportunities specific to their operation
- **For:** Owners curious about AI/automation who don't know where to start
- **Upsell path:** Automation Build Sprint or retainer

### Tech Stack Audit — $497 flat

- **Format:** View access to current tools + intake form → 3-4 page audit + 45-min debrief call
- **Deliverable:** What they have, how it's used vs. how it should be, what's redundant, what's missing, prioritized action list
- **For:** Companies unsure they're getting full value from software they bought, or about to make a new tech investment
- **Upsell path:** Tech stack implementation, Automation Build Sprint, or retainer

### OPS Assessment — $997 flat ⭐ START HERE

- **Format:** 90-min video diagnostic + document review (P&L, FSM walkthrough, org overview) → 4-6 page diagnostic report within 5 business days + 60-min debrief/roadmap call
- **Deliverable:** Prioritized problem matrix, quick wins, 90-day roadmap
- **Credit:** $997 applied toward Month 1 if client signs a retainer within 30 days
- **Why this exists:** Primary gateway to a retainer. Highest-value entry product.
- **Delivery time after templates built:** 10-12 hours

## Group 2: Productized Sprints (3 offers — flat $2,000 each)

Fixed scope, fixed price, defined timeline. Project engagements, not ongoing.

### SOP Sprint — $2,000 flat

- **Format:** 3-4 weeks
- **Scope:** 5 fully documented SOPs for highest-priority processes (process map + written SOP + training guide each)
- **Hours to deliver:** 12-15 (watch scope creep — at 25 hrs this is $80/hr, worse than roofing job)
- **Examples:** Dispatch/scheduling, job closeout, estimate process, complaint handling, onboarding

### Automation Build Sprint — $2,000 flat

- **Format:** 3-4 weeks
- **Scope:** 2-3 automation workflows designed, built, tested, deployed + maintenance docs
- **Hours to deliver:** 15-20
- **Examples:** Lead follow-up, appointment confirmation/reminders, review request, invoice follow-up, weekly reporting pull

### KPI Dashboard Build — $2,000 flat

- **Format:** 2-3 weeks
- **Scope:** Live dashboard on the client's existing tools (revenue, gross margin, close rate, job costing, cash) + auto-report to owner's inbox every Monday
- **Hours to deliver:** 12-18
- **Examples:** Replaces manual number-pulling; one source of truth for the owner

## Group 3: Fractional COO Retainers (4 offers)

### Advisory — $1,500/month

- **Hours:** 4-6/mo, 2 calls/month, async review only
- **Minimum:** 3-month commitment
- **For:** Companies with a capable internal operator who needs strategic guidance, not implementation
- **Effective rate:** $250-375/hr
- **Reality:** Does not transform operations. Helps owners make fewer bad decisions. Do not sell it as transformation.

### Light — $2,500/month

- **Hours:** 8-12/mo, weekly cadence
- **Minimum:** 3-month commitment
- **For:** $2-4M owners beginning to put systems in place
- **Effective rate:** $210-310/hr

### Active — $4,000/month ⭐ MOST COMMON

- **Hours:** 15-20/mo, weekly + project work
- **Minimum:** 6-month commitment
- **For:** $3-10M owners with active build needs across process, tech, accountability
- **Effective rate:** $200-265/hr
- **This is the bread and butter.** Client needs a clear path to $12K+ measurable value within 90 days.

### Embedded — $6,000/month

- **Hours:** 30-40/mo, multiple touchpoints/week
- **Minimum:** 6-12 month commitment
- **For:** $8-20M owners in active growth events (new market, leadership transition, PE prep)
- **Effective rate:** $150-200/hr (the lowest hourly on the ladder — watch scope; this tier earns on depth, not rate)

## Service ladder logic

Entry ($297-997) → Sprint ($2,000) → Retainer ($1,500-6,000/mo)

Goal: $997 OPS Assessment converts at 50-60% to a next-step engagement. If under 40%, the assessment isn't selling the next step properly. Don't present retainer tiers as a menu — diagnose, then propose one.

## Mandatory clauses on every engagement

See [[contract-requirements]]:

- Scope change order policy ($250/hr or $2K per added workflow)
- Out-of-scope explicitly defined
