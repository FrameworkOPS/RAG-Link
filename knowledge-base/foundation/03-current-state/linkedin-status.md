# LinkedIn Status

## Current state

- **Connections:** ~400
- **Posting cadence:** Consistent (recently started)
- **Audience composition:** Mixed — roofing industry, MBA cohort, vendors, some home services adjacent

## Realistic math at this size

- 400 connections × ~30-40% relevant = ~120-160 relevant connections
- Typical organic reach: 5-15% of network on good post
- Good post reaches 20-50 relevant people
- Conversion impression → conversation: well under 1% for cold audiences

**Conclusion: LinkedIn is NOT a lead gen channel at this stage. It's proof-of-expertise infrastructure.**

## What posts must accomplish (next 60 days)

1. **Be findable.** Prospects Google after warm intros. Posts establish credibility.
2. **Build content asset library** — every post repurposable into sales material.
3. **Signal to roofing industry network** that fractional COO is real and active. Earliest clients come from existing relationships.

## Content angles that work

### Build-in-public posts
- ClickUp command center setup
- Engagement template generator builds
- N8N flow specifics
- Why: differentiated, demonstrates the skill being sold, most fractional COOs don't show their work.

### Operator POV posts (highest credibility moat)
- Insights from sitting COO seat at large roofing company
- Real-world examples of what breaks at home service companies
- Why: unique perspective competitors don't have. Use this angle aggressively before leaving the role.

### Tactical breakdowns
- "How we cut onboarding time from 4 hours to 40 minutes"
- Screenshots, numbers, mechanism
- Why: gets saved and shared, attracts ICP-shaped audience.

## Content angles to avoid

- Generic leadership quotes
- AI hype posts
- Motivational content
- "5 lessons I learned" listicles without specifics
- Anything that reads as fluff to a home service owner

## Realistic 60-day outcomes

- Modest connection growth: 400 → 600-800
- Handful of meaningful conversations
- 2-3 warm intros if explicitly asked
- **Zero direct inbound leads worth closing**

That's the realistic baseline. Anything more is upside.

## Where actual pipeline comes from (first 6 months)

1. Existing network (roofing, MBA cohort, vendors)
2. Direct outreach to tight ICP list
3. Daniel becoming case study + referral source

LinkedIn supports these channels. Does not replace them.

## Posting workflow needed (Week 1 deliverable)

- 30-post backlog drafted by end of Week 2
- ClickUp content calendar with status pipeline (Drafted → Scheduled → Published)
- Hook library
- Engagement tracking
