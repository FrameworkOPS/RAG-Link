## Entity question — Carlos and other 1099 income

**Bill through Framework OPS LLC, stay on default tax treatment (single-member disregarded entity = Schedule C).**

S-corp election doesn't pencil at current income:
- SE tax savings ~$3,666 (W-2 reasonable comp $55K, distribution $30.5K)
- QBI deduction loss ~$2,339 (W-2 wages aren't QBI-eligible)
- S-corp overhead ~$1,500–$2,500/yr (payroll service + 1120-S return)
- Net: roughly break-even or slight loss

**Re-evaluate when:** 1099 net income trends past $100K consistently, OR AGI approaches QBI SSTB phaseout (~$394K MFJ).

Non-tax reasons to bill through Framework OPS LLC anyway: liability separation, clean books in QBO via Mercury, brand consistency, sets up future S-corp election cleanly.
