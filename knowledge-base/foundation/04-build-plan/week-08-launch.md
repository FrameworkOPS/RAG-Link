# Week 8 — Soft Launch (June 29 - July 5, 2026)

**Target hours:** ~30
**Sunday checkpoint:** July 5, 7-9pm PT

## *** OUTREACH OFFICIALLY STARTS THIS WEEK ***

## Deliverables

### 1. Warm outreach wave 1 (~10 hrs)
- Contact top 20-30 warm relationships
- Personalized message per [[outreach-infrastructure]]
- Goal: 5+ discovery calls booked

### 2. LinkedIn activation (~6 hrs)
- Daniel case study post series goes live
- DM 20 ICP-adjacent connections personally
- Engage on 10 ICP owner posts daily

### 3. Polish from real reactions (~6 hrs)
- Iterate sales pages, SOWs, pricing based on actual feedback
- Update [[website-status]] with changes

### 4. Buffer (~8 hrs)
- For the thing that broke or took 2x longer
- Catch-up on any Week 1-7 slippage
- DO NOT use this to start new initiatives

## Checkpoint questions

- How many discovery calls booked?
- What feedback is killing deals before they start?
- What's the gap between plan and reality across 8 weeks?
- Are case studies/SOWs/pricing actually working in real conversations?

## 60-Day Mark — Honest Assessment

By Sunday July 5, answer honestly:

- Are you actually doing outreach or still building?
- Is the foundation real, or 70% built and 30% scaffolded?
- Is there a clear path to first paying client outside of Daniel?

## What success looks like

- 5+ discovery calls booked
- 1-2 prospects in active conversation
- All infrastructure tested in production (booking flow, intake form, CRM)
- Daniel case study live and being shared

## What "good enough" looks like

- 3 discovery calls booked
- Outreach started, real responses received
- Some infrastructure gaps remaining but addressable

## What failure looks like

- 0 outreach sent
- "Need another week" mentality
- Infrastructure still 50% built

If hitting failure mode: stop building, force outreach, accept imperfect infrastructure. **Revenue beats readiness.**
