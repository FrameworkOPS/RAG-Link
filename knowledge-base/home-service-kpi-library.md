# Home Service KPI Library

Master list of KPIs for home service companies, organized by department. Not every company needs every KPI — start with 8–12 that match the current business stage. Add more as data infrastructure matures.

---

## Sales KPIs

| KPI | Definition | Target Range | Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Close Rate | Jobs sold / estimates run | 35–60% (varies by trade) | Weekly |
| Average Job Value (AJV) | Total revenue / number of jobs sold | Varies by trade | Monthly |
| Lead-to-Estimate Rate | Estimates scheduled / leads received | 60–80% | Weekly |
| Cost Per Lead (CPL) | Total marketing spend / total leads generated | Trade-dependent | Monthly |
| Cost Per Acquired Customer (CAC) | Total marketing spend / jobs sold | Trade-dependent | Monthly |
| Revenue Per Salesperson | Total revenue generated / number of salespeople | $800K–$1.5M (roofing) | Monthly |
| Pipeline Value | Total estimated value of open quotes not yet won/lost | Use as a forecast | Weekly |
| Quote-to-Close Cycle Time | Days from estimate to decision | Industry-specific | Monthly |
| Win/Loss Ratio by Lead Source | Close rate broken out by lead source | Higher = better ROI per source | Monthly |
| Upsell/Add-on Attach Rate | Jobs with additional services sold / total jobs | 15–35% | Monthly |

## Operations KPIs

| KPI | Definition | Target Range | Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Technician Utilization Rate | Billable hours / available hours | 75–85% | Weekly |
| Jobs Per Day Per Tech | Number of jobs completed per tech | 4–7 (service calls) | Daily/Weekly |
| Callback Rate | Jobs requiring return visit (warranty/error) / total jobs | <3% | Weekly |
| First-Call Resolution Rate | Jobs completed without return visit / total jobs | 85–95% | Weekly |
| On-Time Arrival Rate | Jobs where tech arrived within promised window | >90% | Weekly |
| Same-Day/Next-Day Availability Rate | Customers who get appointment within 24 hours | >85% | Weekly |
| Job Cycle Time | Days from sale to job completion | Trade-specific | Monthly |
| Schedule Fill Rate | Scheduled slots filled / available slots | >85% (off-peak), >95% (peak) | Weekly |
| Equipment/Material Waste Rate | Materials wasted or returned / materials purchased | <12% (roofing) | Per-job review |
| Safety Incidents | OSHA recordable incidents per 100 employees | <2.0 | Monthly |

## Financial KPIs

| KPI | Definition | Target Range | Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gross Margin % | (Revenue - COGS) / Revenue | 38–55% (varies by trade/mix) | Monthly |
| Gross Margin $ Per Job | Gross profit dollars per completed job | Know your floor | Weekly |
| Net Profit Margin % | Net income / revenue | 8–15% | Monthly |
| Overhead Ratio | Total overhead / total revenue | <30% | Monthly |
| Labor as % of Revenue | Total labor cost / revenue | 28–40% | Monthly |
| Materials as % of Revenue | Total materials cost / revenue | 15–40% (trade-dependent) | Monthly |
| Revenue Per Employee | Total revenue / total headcount | $120K–$250K | Quarterly |
| Days Sales Outstanding (DSO) | Avg accounts receivable / (annual revenue / 365) | <35 days | Monthly |
| Cash Conversion Cycle | Days between cash out (materials) and cash in (payment) | Minimize | Monthly |
| Job Cost Variance | Actual job cost vs. estimated job cost | <5% variance | Per-job |
| Revenue per Truck/Vehicle | Total revenue / number of revenue-generating vehicles | $200K–$450K/truck | Monthly |
| Operating Cash Flow | Cash generated by operations before financing | Positive and growing | Monthly |
| WIP (Work in Progress) | Value of work contracted but not yet complete | Track, don't let it age | Weekly |

## People and HR KPIs

| KPI | Definition | Target Range | Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Employee Turnover Rate | Employees who left / average headcount | <25% annual (industry avg is 35–45%) | Monthly |
| Time to Fill Open Position | Days from job posting to accepted offer | <30 days (field); <45 days (office) | Per hire |
| Offer Acceptance Rate | Offers accepted / offers extended | >70% | Monthly |
| 90-Day New Hire Retention | Employees still employed at 90 days / total new hires | >80% | Lagging |
| Training Hours per Employee | Annual training hours per employee | >20 hours/year | Quarterly |
| Overtime as % of Total Labor Hours | OT hours / total labor hours | <15% consistently | Weekly |
| Manager Span of Control | Direct reports per manager | 5–10 for field managers | Quarterly |
| Employee Net Promoter Score (eNPS) | Survey: "Would you recommend this company to work at?" | >30 | Semi-annual |

## KPI Reporting Cadence Recommendation

**Daily (for owner/dispatcher):** Jobs scheduled, jobs completed, tech utilization

**Weekly (leadership review):** Close rate, revenue vs. goal, callback rate, AR aging

**Monthly (business review):** All financial KPIs, turnover, gross margin by service line, NPS, lead source ROI

**Quarterly (strategic review):** Full KPI dashboard, benchmark comparison, trend analysis, goal reset

Do not track everything from day one. Start with: close rate, gross margin %, revenue per tech, callback rate, and DSO. These five will reveal the most about operational health fastest.
