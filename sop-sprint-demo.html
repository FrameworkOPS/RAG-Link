Exception Handling
⚠

"I want to get other quotes"

Response: Do not discount. Ask what specifically they're comparing. Provide the Competitor Comparison one-pager. Schedule a specific callback before leaving. Never leave without a next step.

⚠

Homeowner is not the sole decision maker

Response: Identify this at arrival, before inspection. Reschedule when both decision makers are present, or request a 3-way call during presentation. Do not leave a written estimate with one party expecting a callback.

⚠

Insurance job — scope may change after adjuster

Response: Present as RCV only. Explain the supplement process. Flag as "Insurance – Pending Adjuster" in ServiceTitan. Do not follow the standard close cadence until adjuster visit is complete.

01

Receive & Log Immediately

Any staff member who receives a complaint — by phone, email, text, or in person — logs it in ServiceTitan under the job within 15 minutes. Do not attempt to resolve it on the spot without logging first. Required fields: complaint summary, customer name, job number, date/time received.

OWNER: Any staff → Office MgrLog within 15 min
02

Severity Assessment

Office Manager assesses within 1 hour: High (active leak, structural concern, customer threatening legal or public review) → escalate to owner immediately. Medium (workmanship concern, cleanup issue) → schedule site visit within 48 hrs. Low (billing question, scheduling confusion) → resolve by phone same day.

OWNER: Office ManagerAssessed within 1 hrHIGH → Owner in 15 min
03

Customer Acknowledgment Call

Office Manager calls the customer within 24 hours regardless of severity. Script: acknowledge the issue, confirm you've reviewed the job, give them a specific resolution timeline. Do not promise outcomes you can't deliver. Do not be defensive. The goal of this call is to make them feel heard.

OWNER: Office ManagerWithin 24 hrs
04

Resolution & Root Cause Log

Once resolved, log the resolution method and root cause in ServiceTitan. Root cause options: installer error, material defect, scope miscommunication, weather-related, customer expectation mismatch. This data is reviewed weekly — patterns trigger process changes.

OWNER: Office ManagerLogged before closing ticket
Rule: No complaint is closed without a root cause logged. "One-off" is not a root cause.

Implementation Philosophy

Don't launch all 5 SOPs at once. Stagger by impact and complexity. Sales (SOP-01) and Closeout (SOP-02) go live first — they directly affect revenue collection and customer experience. Dispatch and HR follow in Week 2. Complaints last, because it requires buy-in from the entire team.

Each SOP has a 1-week shadow period where staff follow the new process alongside the old one, then a go-live date where the old process is retired. Framework OPS reviews compliance data weekly during the rollout.

WEEK 1
May 1–7
Training Week — Sales & Closeout

Walk through SOP-SALES-01 and SOP-OPS-01 with the sales team and production coordinator. Confirm ServiceTitan fields and automation triggers are set up correctly. Run through each process step live with the team — no live client jobs yet.
