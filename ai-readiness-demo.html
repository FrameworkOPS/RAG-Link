What This Assessment Does NOT Cover
This assessment identifies where automation can help, not why the underlying operations are struggling. If job costing is inaccurate, automating reporting surfaces bad numbers faster. If the close rate problem is pricing or rep performance, a follow-up sequence won't fix it. AI and automation are force multipliers — they amplify whatever process is already there, good or bad. If you need a full operational diagnosis (people, process, financial, and tech), that's the OPS Assessment ($997).
