How We Build

A process worth your time.

No surprises, no change-order whiplash. Here's how a project actually unfolds from first walk to final walk-through.

I

Discovery

Walk the property, talk about the way you live, set the budget honestly.

II

Design

Architectural plans, finish selections, and a build spec you can hold in your hand.

III

Pre-Construction

Permits, subcontractor bids, schedule lock, and a fixed-price contract.

IV

Build

Weekly site walks, transparent budget tracking, every decision documented.

V

Handoff

Walk-through, punch list, owner's manual, and a one-year warranty you can call us on.
