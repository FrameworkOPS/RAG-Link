## Topics

### The Problem: Founder Dependency

  - [Many owner-led construction firms are trapped in a founder dependency pattern, where the business cannot function without the owner.](https://fathom.video/share/Myz6_E4JeQ5of38WoCTW-oWae9JXHF6D?tab=summary&timestamp=591.0)
  - [**Goal:** Shift these businesses to a leadership-team-led model, enabling the owner to step back and focus on strategy.](https://fathom.video/share/Myz6_E4JeQ5of38WoCTW-oWae9JXHF6D?tab=summary&timestamp=591.0)
  - [**Target Market:** Construction companies, especially MEP (Mechanical, Electrical, Plumbing) trades, with revenue of $5M–$80M.](https://fathom.video/share/Myz6_E4JeQ5of38WoCTW-oWae9JXHF6D?tab=summary&timestamp=2594.0)
      - [**Sweet Spot:** $10M–$30M, where TeamOS can provide a clear growth path.](https://fathom.video/share/Myz6_E4JeQ5of38WoCTW-oWae9JXHF6D?tab=summary&timestamp=2618.0)

### The Solution: TeamOS's Vision & Values

  - [**5-Year Vision:** A new standard for construction: "without dependency."](https://fathom.video/share/Myz6_E4JeQ5of38WoCTW-oWae9JXHF6D?tab=summary&timestamp=1254.0)
      - [**Rationale:** A 5-year horizon is necessary due to rapid AI-driven change (e.g., AI estimating).](https://fathom.video/share/Myz6_E4JeQ5of38WoCTW-oWae9JXHF6D?tab=summary&timestamp=913.0)
      - [**Goal:** Impact 100+ companies in the next 12 months.](https://fathom.video/share/Myz6_E4JeQ5of38WoCTW-oWae9JXHF6D?tab=summary&timestamp=1342.0)
  - [**Core Values ("How We Show Up"):**](https://fathom.video/share/Myz6_E4JeQ5of38WoCTW-oWae9JXHF6D?tab=summary&timestamp=1371.0)
      - [**Golden Rule:** Put yourself in others' shoes.](https://fathom.video/share/Myz6_E4JeQ5of38WoCTW-oWae9JXHF6D?tab=summary&timestamp=1371.0)
      - [**Extra Mile:** Go beyond the scope of work when needed; it balances out.](https://fathom.video/share/Myz6_E4JeQ5of38WoCTW-oWae9JXHF6D?tab=summary&timestamp=1442.0)
      - [**Being Generous:** Share time, energy, and knowledge.](https://fathom.video/share/Myz6_E4JeQ5of38WoCTW-oWae9JXHF6D?tab=summary&timestamp=1678.0)
      - [**Ownership:** Own outcomes; earn the right to work with clients on every call.](https://fathom.video/share/Myz6_E4JeQ5of38WoCTW-oWae9JXHF6D?tab=summary&timestamp=1786.0)
      - [**Truth Over Comfort:** Tell clients what they *need* to hear, not what they *want* to hear.](https://fathom.video/share/Myz6_E4JeQ5of38WoCTW-oWae9JXHF6D?tab=summary&timestamp=1917.0)

### The Differentiator: "Your Team in the Room"

  - [**Construction Native:** Frameworks, scorecards, and cadences are built specifically for construction.](https://fathom.video/share/Myz6_E4JeQ5of38WoCTW-oWae9JXHF6D?tab=summary&timestamp=2009.0)
  - [**Built to Transform:** The goal is measurable business transformation, not just implementing tools.](https://fathom.video/share/Myz6_E4JeQ5of38WoCTW-oWae9JXHF6D?tab=summary&timestamp=2036.0)
      - [**Example:** A client achieved its $15M annual revenue goal by May.](https://fathom.video/share/Myz6_E4JeQ5of38WoCTW-oWae9JXHF6D?tab=summary&timestamp=2066.0)
  - [**Your Team in the Room:** TeamOS works with the entire leadership team (L10s, offsites, peer groups), not just the CEO.](https://fathom.video/share/Myz6_E4JeQ5of38WoCTW-oWae9JXHF6D?tab=summary&timestamp=2127.0)

### The Process: "Diagnosis, Confront, Build"

  - [**Diagnosis:** Identify patterns and key levers for improvement.](https://fathom.video/share/Myz6_E4JeQ5of38WoCTW-oWae9JXHF6D?tab=summary&timestamp=2228.0)
      - [**Example:** A "shallow" Accountability Chart indicated a lack of middle management, which was preventing the leadership team from focusing on strategy.](https://fathom.video/share/Myz6_E4JeQ5of38WoCTW-oWae9JXHF6D?tab=summary&timestamp=2259.0)
  - [**Confront:** Address issues directly, using the "Truth Over Comfort" value.](https://fathom.video/share/Myz6_E4JeQ5of38WoCTW-oWae9JXHF6D?tab=summary&timestamp=2361.0)
      - [**Example:** Shifting a CEO's negative mindset to a positive one created a company-wide culture change.](https://fathom.video/share/Myz6_E4JeQ5of38WoCTW-oWae9JXHF6D?tab=summary&timestamp=2400.0)
  - [**Build the Team:** Coach the entire leadership team, with a guarantee that the owner can take a fully disconnected vacation.](https://fathom.video/share/Myz6_E4JeQ5of38WoCTW-oWae9JXHF6D?tab=summary&timestamp=2507.0)

### The "TeamOS Way": Guiding Principles

  - [**Be the Thermostat, Not the Thermometer:** Calm the room; don't match its temperature.](https://fathom.video/share/Myz6_E4JeQ5of38WoCTW-oWae9JXHF6D?tab=summary&timestamp=2709.0)
  - [**Transformation, Not Tools:** The value is in coaching mastery, not the tools themselves.](https://fathom.video/share/Myz6_E4JeQ5of38WoCTW-oWae9JXHF6D?tab=summary&timestamp=2849.0)

  - [**Behavior Before Results:** Act like the company you want to be today.](https://fathom.video/share/Myz6_E4JeQ5of38WoCTW-oWae9JXHF6D?tab=summary&timestamp=3075.0)
  - [**Speed Over Perfection:** Get version 1 out; the price of greatness is failure.](https://fathom.video/share/Myz6_E4JeQ5of38WoCTW-oWae9JXHF6D?tab=summary&timestamp=3149.0)
  - [**Act Like an Owner:** Make decisions from an owner's perspective.](https://fathom.video/share/Myz6_E4JeQ5of38WoCTW-oWae9JXHF6D?tab=summary&timestamp=3233.0)
  - [**Quality Beats Volume:** Focus on the most important goals and metrics.](https://fathom.video/share/Myz6_E4JeQ5of38WoCTW-oWae9JXHF6D?tab=summary&timestamp=3260.0)
  - [**Care Enough to Tell the Truth:** Be a trusted advisor, not a "cheerleader."](https://fathom.video/share/Myz6_E4JeQ5of38WoCTW-oWae9JXHF6D?tab=summary&timestamp=3288.0)
