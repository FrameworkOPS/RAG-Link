### The Solution: A 30/60/90-Day Plan

  - [**Phase 1: Discovery & Audit (Days 1–30)**](https://fathom.video/share/A-QujF9gJq43H21yyWYZVwE43Ur6J-Ay?tab=summary&timestamp=1236.0)
