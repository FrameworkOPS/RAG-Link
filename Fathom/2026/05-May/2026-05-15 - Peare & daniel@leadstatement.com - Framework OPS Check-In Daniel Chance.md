### Offer & Pricing Strategy

  - [**Context:** The market is saturated with low-quality B2B ad pitches, making a risk-reversal offer necessary to stand out.](https://fathom.video/share/eQhjtpZsnacAMKHVNt6iaiCASSo_kBT5?tab=summary&timestamp=4315.0)
  - [**Dual-Offer Model:**](https://fathom.video/share/eQhjtpZsnacAMKHVNt6iaiCASSo_kBT5?tab=summary&timestamp=4526.0)
      - [**Warm Leads (Referrals, Ads):** Pitch a high-ticket, pay-in-full (PIF) offer (e.g., $6k) to generate significant cash flow.](https://fathom.video/share/eQhjtpZsnacAMKHVNt6iaiCASSo_kBT5?tab=summary&timestamp=4526.0)
      - [**Cold Email:** Pitch a monthly retainer to build predictable recurring revenue (MRR).](https://fathom.video/share/eQhjtpZsnacAMKHVNt6iaiCASSo_kBT5?tab=summary&timestamp=4526.0)
  - [**Cold Email Offer Structure:**](https://fathom.video/share/eQhjtpZsnacAMKHVNt6iaiCASSo_kBT5?tab=summary&timestamp=980.0)
      - [**Initial Idea:** A 14-day free trial.](https://fathom.video/share/eQhjtpZsnacAMKHVNt6iaiCASSo_kBT5?tab=summary&timestamp=980.0)
          - [**Risk:** High upfront work with no guaranteed payment.](https://fathom.video/share/eQhjtpZsnacAMKHVNt6iaiCASSo_kBT5?tab=summary&timestamp=4265.0)
          - [**Solution:** Require a small setup fee (e.g., $2k) to cover initial costs.](https://fathom.video/share/eQhjtpZsnacAMKHVNt6iaiCASSo_kBT5?tab=summary&timestamp=1025.0)
      - [**Revised Idea:** A standard monthly retainer.](https://fathom.video/share/eQhjtpZsnacAMKHVNt6iaiCASSo_kBT5?tab=summary&timestamp=4526.0)
          - [**Benefit:** Stacks MRR and builds a client base that can generate future PIF referrals.](https://fathom.video/share/eQhjtpZsnacAMKHVNt6iaiCASSo_kBT5?tab=summary&timestamp=4581.0)
